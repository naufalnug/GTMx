## Information gain (what a reader cannot get from the top 5 results)

The term is used loosely and nobody has drawn a clean boundary. The gain here is (a) a definition that
states what GTM engineering **is not**, (b) an original framework separating GTM engineering from
RevOps and from growth, with the overlap made explicit, and (c) a synthesis of what the tool vendors
themselves say, which no single post currently pulls together.

This is the pillar. Posts 2, 3 and 4 all link up to it.

## Questions for the expert

Answer in your own words. Specifics beat polish — a half-sentence with a real number is worth more
than a paragraph without one.

1. When did you start calling the work "GTM engineering" rather than something else, and what changed
   that made the old label wrong?
2. What is the clearest example you have of work that is GTM engineering but is **not** RevOps?
3. What do clients most often assume GTM engineering means, and what do they actually get?
4. What was the first thing you built that made you think this is a distinct discipline?
5. What surprised you about how the work differs from running campaigns?
6. One number, if you have one: how long does a typical first build take end to end?
