How this is measured

Method & honest caveats

What the number means

  • Match key is domain. Storylane's HubSpot demo companies were resolved to domains, then matched against the email domains GTMx contacted in the Storylane.io EmailBison workspace.
  • "Influenced" = emailed on or before the booking. If GTMx's first email to a domain predates its Became Demo booked date, GTMx was in the account before the meeting existed.
  • "First emailed" uses lead-added date (EmailBison created_at) as the proxy for when outreach began — the platform doesn't expose an exact first-send timestamp at this scale. Leads are sequenced within a day or two of being added.
  • Coverage is 95.9% of the 42,293 contacted leads (40,568 scanned). A single bulk-import day exceeds the API's pagination ceiling, leaving a small unreachable remainder — so real influence is marginally higher than shown, never lower.
  • Domains are auto-resolved from company names; 126 of 428 couldn't be resolved and are shown separately, never folded silently into the influence rate.
  • This is correlation, not proven causation. A booked demo can have several sources; being emailed first is a strong influence signal, not a claim of exclusive credit.
