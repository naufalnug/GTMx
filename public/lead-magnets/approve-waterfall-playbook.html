Waterfall

One cascade routes every equipment seller

First match wins. Competitor finance widgets go first, then financing mentioned with no embedded apply, then a live catalog with no financing language, then every remaining in-TAM seller.

first match wins 
1. IF in_tam AND finance_page_state = competitor_widget (TimePayment, Navitas, Ascentium, DLL, Leaf, Balboa, Channel, or named single-lender partner on site) -> Tier 1 - Lender-Competition Switch Brief (They already sell with financing and are locked to one lender. Strongest intent for competing offers and higher approval rates.)
2. IF in_tam AND finance_page_state = mention_no_embed (financing / leasing / monthly payments mentioned, no apply widget or branded program) -> Tier 2 - POS Embed Brief (They already believe financing closes deals, but the buyer still has to ask. Pain is drop-off, not education.)
3. IF in_tam AND finance_page_state = catalog_no_financing (product catalog, shop, or quote flow live, zero financing language) -> Tier 3 - 82% Capture Brief (Buyers are shopping equipment with no payment path. Maps to the 82% ELFA figure on approvepayments.com.)
4. IF in_tam AND none of the above -> Catch-all - Seller Finance Playbook (Still in TAM (equipment manufacturers, distributors, dealers). Softest CTA, broadest copy, mandatory 100% coverage.)
