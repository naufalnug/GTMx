Prepared for SMADE

The SMADE waterfall journey.

One priority-routed cascade across every medical device manufacturer with an instrument fleet in circulation, built entirely on free federal device data.

Core insight: A manufacturer's instrument set fleet is the largest capital line item they control that nobody measures. It sits on the balance sheet as a named asset, depreciates over five years, and gets written off when it disappears. Yet how many sets to build is decided once, at launch, by a person guessing. One SMADE client planned a five-set build; the data showed they needed two.

The signal

The routing signal nobody can buy

Every account, every tier and every lead magnet comes out of the FDA's own device databases. Qualification happens before a single enrichment credit is spent.

strongest signal
01

AccessGUDID publishes a device-kit flag and a single-use flag on every device record, so you can prove which companies actually own reusable instrument fleets rather than guessing from an industry code.

02

The openFDA 510(k) database timestamps every clearance, which is the exact moment a manufacturer commits capital to a new set fleet. It is a launch clock, published free, updated monthly.

03

No data vendor sells this. Purchased medtech lists cannot separate a disposable device company from one running two thousand trays, because the distinction lives in the device record rather than the company record.

04

It replenishes itself. Roughly fifty new qualified accounts arrive every month at the precise moment their fleet sizing decision is live, and none of them had to be bought.

01

AccessGUDID Full Release

The base market. Every device on the US market plus labeler name, brand, device-kit flag, single-use flag, sterilisation flag and FDA product code. Bulk download, roughly four million records, free and no account required.

02

openFDA Registration & Listing API

The corporate entity behind each device. Registered establishment name, address, country, owner or operator, and official correspondent. Free.

03

openFDA 510(k) API

The Tier 1 trigger. New clearances with applicant, decision date and product code. This is the launch clock that tells you a set fleet is about to be built.

04

EUDAMED actor and UDI-DI registry

The European mirror of GUDID, mandatory since 28 May 2026. Extends the same qualification gate across the EU market.

05

Company domain crawl

Rep-locator, distributor, territory and loaner pages, revealing whether the fleet is handled by employees or by independent reps, and roughly how many territories it spreads across.

06

Job-board and vendor-name sweep

Open requisitions for set, loaner, field-inventory and asset-management coordination, plus any named incumbent tracking vendor mentioned in postings, press or integration pages.

07

SEC EDGAR full-text search

For public filers, the instruments-and-sets balance sheet line and depreciation policy. Powers the Balance Sheet 100 account-based campaign, where you can open with the prospect's own filed number.

The waterfall

One cascade, routed on the set-fleet capital cycle

Accounts are tested top to bottom and land in exactly one tier, so nobody ever receives two competing messages.

first match wins 
1. IF The account publishes no reusable and no kit device records -> Exclude from the market entirely (No fleet means nothing to track and nothing to buy. This gate removes every single-use and disposable device company, which is the filter no purchased list can apply.)
2. IF The account is headquartered in Germany -> Suppress from email, route to a LinkedIn and phone lane (UWG section 7 requires prior consent for commercial email, including B2B. Tuttlingen is the densest surgical instrument cluster on earth, so these accounts are worked on other channels rather than dropped.)
3. IF A 510(k) or CE clearance landed in the trailing twelve months -> Tier 1, Fleet Being Built (The set-count decision is live right now, and whatever number they pick depreciates for five years whether the sets move or not.)
4. IF A named incumbent tracking or RFID vendor is detected -> Tier 2, Visibility Stops at the Dock (They already pay for tracking and already believe in the category, so the only question left is the gap, and the gap is provable.)
5. IF The company publishes an independent or hybrid rep network on its own site -> Tier 3, Fleet in Other People's Cars (Assets sit in the custody of people who do not work for them, so location data comes back as a favour rather than as a fact.)
6. IF There is at least one open requisition for set, loaner, field-inventory or asset-management coordination -> Tier 4, Fleet Managed by Headcount (They have already priced the problem and approved budget for it, just in salary and spreadsheets rather than in data.)
7. IF None of the above matched -> Tier 5 catch-all, The Unmeasured Fleet (The fleet exists and turn rate has never been measured, so dormant trays are invisible and indistinguishable from busy ones.)

The launch

Sixteen weeks to steady state, then one refresh a month

Ongoing maintenance is a single monthly data pull. The market refreshes itself, for free, forever.

launch first
01

Weeks 1 to 3, build. Pull the AccessGUDID full release, the openFDA Registration & Listing data and the 510(k) database, then apply the qualification gate so only companies with a provable instrument fleet enter the market. Stand up four to six secondary sending domains and begin a twenty-one day warmup.

02

Weeks 4 to 6, Tier 1 goes live alone. Roughly 2,500 contacts against the highest-intent trigger in the programme. It gives the fastest read and it validates the entire free-data pipeline end to end before any volume is added.

03

Weeks 6 to 9, add Tier 3. The rep-locator crawl runs in parallel with Phase 1 so the list is ready the moment the phase opens. This is the biggest high-signal tier and the volume engine.

04

Weeks 9 to 13, add Tier 4 and then open the catch-all. This is the volume phase, and it is where the strategy starts compounding across the full market.

05

Weeks 13 to 16, launch Tier 2 once the vendor sweep clears 1,500 contacts, and begin the Balance Sheet 100 manually alongside it to CFO, Controller and VP FP&A.

06

Week 16 onward, steady state. Re-pull 510(k) monthly for roughly fifty new Tier 1 accounts, re-run the job sweep weekly, and age accounts out of Tier 1 into their next matching tier. Ongoing maintenance is one data refresh a month.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacts Perfect. Scale the tier hard. Tiers 1 and 2 should land at or near this, because one is precisely timed and the other is a confirmed budget holder.
1 per 300 Good. Let it run unchanged. This is the expected landing zone for Tiers 3 and 4.
1 per 450 to 500 Borderline. Test exactly one copy variant, varying the magnet or the notice-line angle and holding everything else constant so the result is attributable.
Worse than 1 per 500, or reply rate below 1% after 1,500 or more sends Failing. Cut the tier and rework it. The one exception is Tier 5, where 1 per 400 remains acceptable because the contact cost is effectively zero.
