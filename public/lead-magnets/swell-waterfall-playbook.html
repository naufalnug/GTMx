Waterfall

One cascade routes every DSO, MSO, and specialty group

First match wins. Named Birdeye / Podium / Rater8 / Reputation.com / SocialClimb goes first, then multi-location Google listings with unanswered or thin reviews, then PX/NPS language with no named competitor, then every remaining in-TAM multi-location healthcare group.

Strongest intent for Swell's purpose-built pitch: swellcx.com/online-reputation contrasts Swell vs Birdeye/Podium (retail templates, flat accounts, compliance bolted on) and cites 5× more reviews and a 4.92 rating across 12,000+ locations.)
3. IF in_tam AND reputation_stack_state = listings_unanswered_or_thin (multi-location Google / Healthgrades listings live, reviews unanswered or review volume thin vs peer clinics, no named competitor tool) -> Tier 2 - Review Velocity Brief (The listings exist; the motion does not. Pain is inferred from the trigger: office managers chasing reviews by hand, a regional ops director who cannot read 8,000 reviews a quarter (homepage), unanswered reviews that stick before a rating drop hits Google.)
4. IF in_tam AND reputation_stack_state = px_language_no_competitor (patient-experience / NPS / CSAT / survey language on site, no named competitor, not the unanswered/thin-listing case) -> Tier 3 - PX Command Center Brief (They already talk PX, but public reviews and private surveys are not on one data layer.
