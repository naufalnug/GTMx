Waterfall

One cascade routes every in-TAM travel & hospitality account

First match wins. Competing ESG platform first, then certified-without-platform, then sustainability-page-without-cert, then every remaining hotel, group, destination, and travel partner in TAM.

first match wins 
1. IF in_tam AND sustainability_maturity = competitor_platform (Ecostars, Con-Serve/Considerate, Sustainwize, GreenTurGo, or adjacent hospitality ESG data hub) AND not already_customer -> Tier 1 - Competitor Switch + Consolidation Brief (They already budget a hospitality ESG/sustainability platform. Strongest switch intent for BeCause as the travel-native data hub with 60+ framework mapping and open sharing to OTAs/partners.)
2. IF in_tam AND sustainability_maturity = certified_no_platform (public Green Key / Travelife / GSTC-aligned / EarthCheck / GreenSign / Nordic Swan / Booking.com Travel Sustainable / similar) AND no Tier-1 competitor_platform AND not already_customer -> Tier 2 - Framework Mapping Brief (They already carry a public eco-cert or Travel Sustainable badge but no competing hub — classic duplicate-reporting pain across frameworks, RFPs, and OTAs. Maps to BeCause enter-once / map-everywhere.)
3. IF in_tam AND sustainability_maturity = sustainability_page_no_cert (sustainability/ESG/CR page OR Head of Sustainability / ESG title) AND no Tier 1-2 AND not already_customer -> Tier 3 - Certification Co-pilot Brief (They signal intent publicly but have not landed a cert yet. Maps to BeCause Certification Co-pilot + CSRD/ESRS prep without inventing a cert they do not have.)
4. IF in_tam AND none of the above -> Catch-all - BeCause Sustainability Data Playbook (Still in TAM (hotels, groups, destinations, travel partners). Softest CTA around consolidating sustainability data, certifications, and partner sharing. Mandatory 100% coverage.)
