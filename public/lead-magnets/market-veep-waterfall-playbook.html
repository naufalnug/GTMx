Prepared for Market Veep

The Market Veep outbound journey.

One waterfall, one crawl, four campaigns, and 100% of the manufacturing market covered.

Core insight: Market Veep's whole service ladder maps onto one publicly observable variable: the state of the prospect's revenue stack, readable straight from their website's page source at zero enrichment cost. HubSpot's script, forms, CMS assets and meetings links all render client-side, so a single crawl tells us whether a manufacturer owns HubSpot, whether they actually use the HubSpot they own, what else is running beside it, or whether they're stuck on a legacy stack. That variable orders perfectly by buying intent, and each state of it points at a different Market Veep service.

02

How every account gets routed

Accounts are tested top to bottom and land in the first tier they match. One account, one campaign, no overlap, no gaps.

first match wins 
1. IF HubSpot is detected AND a competing system is also detected -> Tier 1 - Two systems, one revenue team (Two systems in use means two versions of the truth, and a reconciliation cost somebody is paying in hours every week.)
2. IF HubSpot is detected AND the depth score is 1 or lower -> Tier 2 - Paying for a portal you are not using (The script fires but no forms, CMS or meetings links exist. The portal was bought and never deployed.)
3. IF No HubSpot AND (a legacy stack is detected OR a growth signal is present) -> Tier 3 - You have outgrown the stack you are on (They are spending on growth on top of tooling that cannot attribute it, and are often about to hire a person to fix a systems problem.)
4. IF Anything else in the market -> Tier 4 - Catch-all - Your buyers cannot find you (No detectable marketing infrastructure means no compounding pipeline. The business runs on referrals, reps and trade shows.)

Launch sequence and thresholds

Start with the smallest, hottest tier. Prove the model in 10 to 14 days, then phase the rest in.

launch first
01

Weeks 1-2: build the crawler first. Page-source detection for HubSpot depth scoring and competing-system identification blocks both of the hottest tiers, so nothing else starts until it runs.

02

Weeks 1-2: launch Tier 1 to 1,500 contacts. Smallest list, clearest pain, shortest path to a reply that proves the model. Requires the integration map built for Salesforce, Marketo, Mailchimp, Zoho and NetSuite, which covers roughly 85% of the tier.

03

Weeks 3-4: add Tier 2 at +2,500 contacts. Second-highest intent and the strongest lead magnet in the strategy. The 12-point audit must first be productised into a repeatable sub-30-minute process with a templated scored PDF, or fulfilment cost eats the tier.

04

Weeks 5-7: add Tier 3 at +5,000 contacts. Broader, and it needs the growth-signal enrichment (job scrape plus Google Ads Transparency Center) running cleanly before it sends.

05

Week 8 onward: scale Tier 4 to volume. Requires the keyword-gap report generator to be near-zero marginal cost, since this tier is 65% of the market.

06

Do not lead with Tier 4 even though it holds the most accounts. It has the weakest signal, and a weak first result poisons confidence in the whole programme before the strong tiers get a turn.

07

Infrastructure throughout: 4-6 secondary domains, never the root domain. 3 mailboxes per domain, warmed 3+ weeks. 25-30 sends per mailbox per day. Link and open tracking off, since reply rate is the only metric that matters and pixels cost deliverability.

08

Once the waterfall is stable, layer side campaigns: new marketing leader job-changes, trade show exhibitor lists (IMTS, FABTECH, Pack Expo), and post-acquisition stack consolidation.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacts Excellent. Scale this tier and pull more market into it.
1 per 300 Good. Let it run untouched.
1 per 450-500 Borderline. Test one copy variant: change the lead magnet, hold everything else constant.
Worse than 1 per 500, or reply rate below 1% after 1,500+ sends Failing. Cut the tier and rework it.
First meaningful read 10-14 days from Tier 1 launch, or roughly 1,500 sends on any tier.
