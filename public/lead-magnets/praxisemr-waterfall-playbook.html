Signal

The signal is the price a practice already pays for template charting

A practice hiring scribes, losing MIPS points, or running a pick-list EHR is showing the cost of templates in public data. NPPES, CMS Care Compare, job boards and the practice's own patient-portal link expose it without paid enrichment.

strongest signal
01

Template pain is invisible in firmographics but visible in behavior: a scribe or transcription job post means the practice is paying people to make up for the EHR.

02

MIPS final scores are published by CMS per group and clinician, so reporting pain is observable and tied to real Medicare payment adjustments, which is what Datum+ automates.

03

The patient-portal link on a practice website (healow, athenaPatient, NextGen Patient Portal, FollowMyHealth, etc.) names the current EHR reliably, because the link only exists if the practice uses that system.

04

All three signals ride on free or cheap public data (NPPES, CMS Care Compare / QPP, job boards, a homepage scrape), so the TAM and the routing signal come in the same pull.

NPPES NPI Registry (CMS, free bulk download)

TAM gate: every U.S. organization NPI with physician taxonomy, practice address, phone, and the authorized official's name and title (often the physician-owner or practice administrator).

02

CMS Care Compare: Doctors and Clinicians national file

Group practice IDs, clinicians per group (provider_count), and hospital affiliations used to exclude health-system-owned clinics.

03

CMS QPP / Care Compare MIPS public reporting

MIPS final score and payment adjustment per group or clinician for the latest published performance year. Routing signal for Tier 2.

04

Indeed + LinkedIn Jobs scrape

Open roles in the last 60 days titled medical scribe, remote scribe, medical transcriptionist, chart prep or clinical documentation specialist. Routing signal for Tier 1.

05

Google Maps + practice homepage scrape

Practice website and the patient-portal link that names the current EHR (current_ehr), plus Praxis portal detection for the exclusion rule. Routing signal for Tier 3.

06

Apollo / LinkedIn Sales Navigator

Verified emails for Office Manager, Practice Manager, Practice Administrator and physician-owner contacts at each routed practice.

Waterfall

First match wins, top to bottom, catch-all underneath

Exclude existing Praxis users and health-system-owned clinics, then route: scribe hiring, then a below-threshold MIPS score, then a detected template EHR, then every other independent practice. Each practice lands in exactly one campaign.

first match wins 
1. IF praxis_customer = true (Praxis portal or Praxis named on site) -> Exclude (Already a Praxis practice. Route to Praxis account management, not cold outbound.)
2. IF health_system_affiliation = owned / employed (Epic MyChart under a hospital domain, or group billed under a health system) -> Exclude (Praxis's own comparison notes less presence in large hospital systems. Employed physicians do not choose their EHR.)
3. IF scribe_job_posted_60d = true -> Tier 1: Scribe spend (The practice is paying people to absorb charting time. Strongest, most time-bound intent.)
4. IF ELSE IF mips_final_score below the CMS performance threshold OR mips_payment_adjustment negative (latest published year) -> Tier 2: MIPS gap (Reporting is costing Medicare revenue. Datum+ automates MIPS, MACRA, ACO and eCQM reporting from the physician's own text.)
5. IF ELSE IF current_ehr in {eClinicalWorks, athenahealth, NextGen, Greenway, Veradigm/Allscripts, Practice Fusion, Amazing Charts, other template-based ambulatory EHR} -> Tier 3: Template EHR switch (Named template EHR on the portal link. Switch framing: Praxis imports demographics from any EHR and transfers charts via C-CDA.)
6. IF ELSE (every remaining independent practice in the TAM) -> Tier 4: Catch-all, chart in your own words (Covers 100% of the TAM. Broad Concept Processing plus physician-rating proof, softest ask.)
