Waterfall

One cascade routes every in-TAM aviation operator

First match wins. Named competitor stack first, then digital portal without a named ERP, then multi-entity / multi-station scale, then every remaining in-TAM aviation company.

first match wins 
1. IF in_tam AND competitor_stack detected (CHAMP Cargospot, IBS iCargo, Softaire, Hits, Mercator, or peer named on site/careers/integrations) AND not already_customer -> Tier 1 - Switch / Side-by-Side Brief (They already pay a competing aviation ERP or cargo CMS. Strongest intent for an Awery switch using integrated ops+finance and published customer proof (100+ customers, Wings 25% productivity).)
2. IF in_tam AND has_booking_portal = true (cargo booking, charter quote, or customer track portal) AND no Tier-1 competitor_stack -> Tier 2 - Digital Ops Consolidation Brief (They already sell digitally but the back-office ERP is unspecified. Maps to Awery ERP + CargoBooking connectivity and Kaylee's connectivity angle.)
3. IF in_tam AND multi_entity_signal = true (multi-station, multi-airline GSA principals, multi-company footprint) AND no Tier 1-2 match -> Tier 3 - Multi-Entity ERP Brief (Scale without a locked competitor stack. Awery's multi-company / multi-currency ERP and ~45 GSA customers are the proof path.)
4. IF in_tam AND none of the above -> Catch-all - Aviation Ops Playbook (Still in TAM (aviation operators Awery lists on awery.aero). Softest CTA around workflow, compliance, and one-platform ops+finance. Mandatory 100% coverage.)
