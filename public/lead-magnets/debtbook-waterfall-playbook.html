01 · Signal

The filing is the signal

We do not enrich. We read the audited document the buyer publishes annually, for free, on a schedule announced in advance.

strongest signal
01

It is statutory. Entities do not stop filing, so the list refreshes itself annually for free and decays at close to zero.

02

It carries five variables at once — debt outstanding, GASB 87/96 exposure, auditor findings, filing lateness, and the signatory's contact details — from a single document.

03

It is unfakeable. 'Your FY2025 report went up on EMMA forty-one days past the undertaking date' is a sentence no competitor's sequence will ever contain.

04

The moat is operational, not technical. Any competitor could read EMMA. None do, because it means parsing thousands of PDFs instead of paying an enrichment vendor.

MSRB EMMA (emma.msrb.org)

The core TAM. Every issuer, every series ever sold, every annual filing and its date, plus rating-change and failure-to-file event notices. Free, refreshed weekly.

02

The ACFR PDF itself

Outstanding debt totals, long-term debt notes, GASB 87 lease liability, GASB 96 SBITA liability or its conspicuous absence, the auditor's report and findings, and the letter of transmittal carrying the Finance Director's name, title, phone and often email.

California CDIAC Report of Final Sale and Annual Debt Transparency Report, Texas Bond Review Board, NC Local Government Commission agendas and equivalents — structured issuance data with issuer contacts already normalised.

04

Bond sale calendars & preliminary Official Statements

Entities with debt about to hit the books in the next 30-90 days — the forward-looking Tier 2 trigger.

05

Government job boards

GovernmentJobs.com, state league-of-cities boards and the GFOA career centre: open reqs for Treasury Manager, Debt Manager, Controller and Accountant.

06

Council, commission and board agendas

New Finance Director appointments, interim appointments, ERP and software purchase approvals, debt authorisations — all published in advance.

07

GFOA Certificate list + state auditor portals

Reporting-maturity score, single audit findings, and in many states an explicit published list of late or delinquent filers.

08

Entity finance-department directories

Persona expansion beyond the ACFR signatory, plus the email naming convention. Public staff directories are more complete than private-sector equivalents because accessibility is often statutory.

09

US Census of Governments

Universe frame and segment sizing — roughly 90,000 local government units, the denominator that shows what has not yet been covered.

02 · Waterfall

One cascade, first match wins

Every account is tested top to bottom and routed to the first tier it matches, so each entity receives exactly one campaign.

first match wins 
1. IF A failure-to-file notice exists on EMMA, OR the ACFR was posted after the undertaking deadline, OR the auditor reported a material weakness or significant deficiency touching debt, capital assets, leases or the close process -> Tier 1 — Filing Distress (The problem is already on the record and visible to the council, the rating agencies and the bondholders. Somebody has already been asked to fix it.)
2. IF At least one new series was issued in the trailing 12 months, OR a refunding occurred, OR a sale is on the calendar within 90 days -> Tier 2 — Fresh Debt on the Books (Every new series is a new tab, a new amortisation schedule and a new set of fund allocations. Refundings are worse — the lineage has to be tracked or the footnote quietly stops tying out.)
3. IF A GASB 87 lease liability is disclosed AND no GASB 96 SBITA liability is disclosed, or it is reported as zero -> Tier 3 — GASB 96 Exposure Gap (They did the lease standard and stopped. The subscription inventory has never been built, because IT signs the software contracts and finance has to report them, with no handoff between.)
4. IF An open finance requisition exists, OR the Finance Director has been in seat under six months, OR the appointment is interim -> Tier 4 — Seat Just Changed Hands (The spreadsheet's author has left or is leaving. A new director inherits a model they did not build and cannot audit. Highest openness to change of any tier.)
5. IF None of the above and outstanding debt is $10M or greater -> Tier 5 — Scale Without a System (catch-all) (No acute event. Sequenced on the benchmark artifact and the peer proof that roughly 40% of the largest 250 municipalities and counties have already moved.)

06 · Launch

Six weeks to steady state

Warmup starts before everything else. Twenty-eight days of it is the critical path into government mail gateways.

launch first
01

Week 0 — Infrastructure. Register 4-6 dedicated sending domains (none of them debtbook.com), configure SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MTA-STS and TLS-RPT, create 12-18 mailboxes and start warmup. This runs before everything else because 28 days of warmup is the critical path into government mail gateways.

02

Week 1 — Core data build. EMMA issuer extraction across all fifty states. Working universe defined and deduped. State registry cross-reference for CA, TX, NC, FL and NY.

03

Week 2 — Enrichment from filings. ACFR PDF ingestion and parsing: debt totals, GASB 87 and 96 balances, filing dates against undertaking deadlines, auditor findings, and transmittal-letter contacts. All routing fields populated.

04

Week 3 — Routing and copy sign-off. Waterfall executed and tier counts reported against estimate. Copy approved by DebtBook including every bracketed figure. Artifacts built — footnote rebuild template, SBITA checklist, handover checklist, benchmark, budget justification memo.

05

Week 4 — Pilot. Tiers 1 and 2 only, 800 contacts, 250/day. Placement seeded into real .gov and .us municipal mailboxes, not commercial seeds. Every single reply reviewed manually.

06

Week 5 — Scale. Tiers 3 and 4 open, volume to 900/day. Tier 5 opens at week 6, deliberately throttled.

07

Week 6+ — Operate. Monthly re-routing as filings and job posts refresh, so a Tier 5 account that posts a Treasury Manager req becomes Tier 4 with a genuinely new reason to write. Weekly placement testing. Fortnightly copy iteration on the worst-performing tier only.

08

Guardrails throughout — open tracking disabled entirely, no links before email three, plain text only, 20-25 sends per mailbox per day, Tier 1 on its own domain pool, and every email written on the assumption it will be read by the recipient, their director and a reporter.

Scale / cut rules

Hard bounce >2.0% Auto-pause the offending domain immediately. Government gateways punish bounce spikes far harder than commercial ones, and recovery takes weeks.
Spam complaints >0.08% Pause the tier, re-read the copy against the public-records test, and re-seed before resuming.
Blended reply <4% The routing is wrong, not the copy. Re-check the ACFR parse before touching a single subject line.
Blended reply ≥7% At target. Raise volume toward the 1,800/day ceiling and open Tier 5 wider.
Tier 1 reply ≥12% Expand the trigger definition — add auditor findings on close and capital assets that were initially scoped out.
Tier 5 reply <2% Throttle rather than iterate. This is the reputation-sensitive tier and it is judged over quarters, not weeks.
Positive replies ≥3% of contacts Scale the tier and pull the budget-justification offer earlier in the sequence.
Meetings <0.8% of contacts reached The offer is wrong for the tier. Swap the artifact before rewriting copy.
Quarter one Steer on qualified pipeline created, never closed-won. Purchase requires a budget line and often a council agenda item, so closed revenue in the first two quarters is a lagging indicator of nothing.
