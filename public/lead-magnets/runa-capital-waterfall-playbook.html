02

How every founder gets routed

One hard gate for stage and geography, then five conditions tested top-down. First match wins. Every account exits with exactly one tier.

first match wins 
1. IF HARD GATE: last round is none, angel, pre-seed or seed - and NOT a seed closed more than 6 months ago - and HQ is in an active Runa geography -> Enter the waterfall. Anything failing the gate is excluded entirely. (Too late, or out of thesis. A company past its Series A is not a first-cheque opportunity, and emailing it burns brand for nothing.)
2. IF Repo has 1,000+ stars, crossed 1k within 18 months, 90-day growth in the top quartile of the ROSS cohort, and 5+ active contributors -> Tier 1 - ROSS Velocity (Developer adoption is compounding faster than the commercial motion. This is the rarest and most predictive signal available, and the only one Runa can speak to with an instrument it built itself.)
3. IF Not Tier 1, and the company is in quantum, chips, photonics, AI infrastructure or robotics, with a founder research or patent footprint, founded within 36 months -> Tier 2 - Pre-Commercial Deeptech (Generalist seed funds soft-pass these founders on 'no traction'. Runa underwrites pre-commercial science, so the same company that is unfundable elsewhere is in thesis here.)
4. IF Not Tier 1 or 2, and live docs, a sandbox or a status page is detected, or a regulatory licence filing is found -> Tier 3 - Fintech Infrastructure (A shipped developer surface or a licence in flight proves this is rails, not an app-layer wrapper. Runa has taken infrastructure to scale on both continents.)
5. IF Not Tier 1, 2 or 3, and the company is posting its first AE, head of sales or first marketer -> Tier 4 - First GTM Hire (Founder-led sales is ending. The next three quarters decide whether the motion is repeatable, and that is what the Series A is decided on.)
6. IF Everything else that clears the hard gate and sits inside the four thesis pillars -> Tier 5 - Catch-All, Thesis Match (No live signal, but still in thesis. Skipping the catch-all leaves 58% of the market on the table and breaks the waterfall's 100% coverage property.)

04

The campaign set

Each tier gets one core campaign. Four side campaigns sit alongside the waterfall on orthogonal triggers - the ROSS publication moment is the highest-converting single send Runa has.

volume + side quests
Core Niche

ROSS Percentile Card

The flagship campaign. Runa is the only fund on earth that can credibly tell an open-source founder where their star curve ranks historically, because Runa built and published the index that measures it. The give is a percentile card plus what the companies at that percentile did next. No meeting attached.

Filter
Pre-Series-A open-source companies whose repo crossed 1,000 stars within 18 months and whose 90-day star growth sits in the top quartile of the ROSS historical cohort, with 5+ active contributors.
AI strategy
Score every candidate repo with Runa's own ROSS methodology off the free GitHub API, then open the email with their actual numbers: month they crossed 1k, stars added in 90 days, active contributor count. The personalisation is computed, not written, so it scales without losing specificity.
Value
Give them the benchmark nobody else has
Core Focused

Pre-Commercial Diligence Map

Deeptech founders waste quarters pitching funds structurally unable to price pre-commercial science. Runa's Pasqal position and Fund IV's explicit future-of-computing mandate make this a genuine mismatch correction, not a pitch. The give is the nine-point map of what Runa underwrites when there is no revenue line.

Filter
Quantum, chips, photonics, AI-infrastructure and robotics companies under 36 months old whose founders carry an arXiv publication or granted patent.
AI strategy
Open on the actual paper or patent by name, then connect it to the spin-out date. The poke-the-bear line names the exact rejection these founders keep hearing - 'come back when there's revenue' - which is the fastest way to prove the sender understands their position.
Value
Reach the fund that can actually underwrite you
Core Focused

Rails Round-Readiness

Infrastructure has a long, expensive path to first design partner plus regulatory drag. Mambu, Zopa and Lendio give Runa the standing to say it underwrites that timeline rather than being surprised by it. The give is a seed-versus-A readiness checklist for rails companies whose revenue lags the build.

Filter
Pre-Series-A fintech companies with a live docs, api, sandbox or status subdomain detected, or a licence application visible on a regulator register.
AI strategy
Read the actual documentation and name a specific endpoint or primitive in the first line. This is the single hardest first line in the whole waterfall to fake, and it instantly separates the sender from every generalist fund emailing the same founder.
Value
Someone who already knows your timeline
Core Broad

The First Commercial Hire

Runa's stated value-add between seed and A is hiring expertise and a transatlantic network across six offices. This tier is where that claim is most directly useful, because the founder is actively in the problem. The give is benchmark data on when the first commercial hire lands relative to the round.

Filter
Pre-Series-A enterprise software companies posting their first AE, head of sales, or first marketer. Refreshed weekly; posts decay in about 45 days.
AI strategy
Trigger on the job post itself and quote a detail from it. The insight to lead with is that the hire IS the experiment - the next three quarters decide whether founder-led sales becomes repeatable, and that is exactly what the Series A gets decided on.
Value
Help with the hire, not just the cheque
Core Broad

We Track, We Don't Chase

The reservoir. Deliberately low volume because this tier is a brand asset rather than a volume play. It converts on a twelve-month horizon, not a two-week one, and its purpose is to be already-known when the founder eventually does open a process.

Filter
Every remaining pre-Series-A company inside the four thesis pillars and Runa's active geographies. Roughly 58% of the addressable market.
AI strategy
One line of genuine product-specific observation, then explicitly remove the pressure: not asking whether you're raising. The give is a standing subscription to the ROSS Index and the quarterly benchmark library - a reason to stay in contact that costs the founder nothing.
Value
Meet the fund before you need one
Side campaign Niche

The ROSS Publication Moment

Not part of the waterfall - it is a calendar-triggered side campaign. But it is almost certainly the single highest-converting outbound moment available to the fund, because the recipient has just been publicly recognised by the sender.

Filter
Every company ranked in the quarter's index that is not already in the portfolio, plus the twenty companies that just missed the cut.
AI strategy
Time the send to the index dropping. For the near-misses, the line writes itself: you were number 23. Four sends a year, each with a genuine, newsworthy, non-salesy reason to be in the inbox.
Value
The highest-converting send Runa has
Side campaign Niche

Portfolio-Adjacent Referral

An orthogonal trigger that sits alongside the waterfall. Low volume by nature, but the highest conversion per send of anything outside the ROSS moment.

Filter
Companies that a Runa portfolio company has hired from, or integrates with.
AI strategy
Use the shared connection as the first line. Continuous, very low volume, and near-warm rather than genuinely cold.
Value
Borrowed trust
Side campaign Focused

Conference-Triggered

Time-boxed and event-driven. Runs per event rather than continuously, and should be suppressed against any tier the account already sits in.

Filter
Speaker and sponsor lists from KubeCon, NeurIPS, Money20/20 and Web Summit.
AI strategy
A two-week pre-event window and a one-week post-event window. Speaking slots are a public, dated, thesis-relevant signal that nobody has to be enriched to reveal.
Value
A reason that expires

Launch, measure, scale

Tier 1 first at 60/day to prove the tone contract before anything touches volume. Negative sentiment under 0.4% outranks reply rate as the go/no-go metric.

launch first
01

Weeks 1-3, Tier 1 only at 60 sends/day. Smallest and highest-conviction tier, and the ROSS asset is effectively already built. This phase exists to prove the tone contract at low brand risk before anything touches volume.

02

Build the ROSS percentile scoring script first: GitHub API plus the historical ROSS dataset, returning a percentile and a peer cohort per company. Runa already owns both the methodology and the data.

03

Weeks 4-6, add Tiers 2 and 3 at 150 sends/day. Both require their one-page lead magnet written first - roughly 45 minutes of a partner's time each.

04

Weeks 7-10, add Tier 4 at 300 sends/day. Requires the weekly Wellfound and LinkedIn job-post refresh job to be running, since posts decay in about 45 days.

05

Week 11 onward, add Tier 5 at 450 sends/day. Deliberately slow. This tier converts on a twelve-month horizon and is managed as a brand asset.

06

Infrastructure: three to four secondary domains, twelve to sixteen mailboxes, four-week warmup, hard cap of 25-30 sends per mailbox per day regardless of tier volume.

07

Partner mailboxes sit on separate infrastructure from the sending domains, with every reply routed to a named partner. Suppress the existing portfolio, live pipeline, and anything already in process.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacts Scale the tier immediately. This is the north star for Tier 1.
1 per 300 Healthy. Let it run untouched and resist the urge to tune.
1 per 450-500 Borderline. Ship the second copy variant before touching the trigger.
Worse than 1 per 500, or reply rate under 1% after 1,500 sends Cut the tier and rework the trigger, not the copy.
Unsubscribes plus negative replies above 0.4% Stop scaling immediately, regardless of reply rate. The copy has drifted salesy and the brand cost outruns any deal this sources.
Magnet fulfilment to first call below 22% The give isn't genuinely valuable. Rebuild the asset before adding volume.
Cold-sourced companies raising a priced round within 12 months below 15% Signal quality problem, not a copy problem. Re-tune the tier triggers.
