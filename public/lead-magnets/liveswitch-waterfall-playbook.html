02 Waterfall

First-match routing, top to bottom

Every qualified account exits with exactly one tier. No overlaps, no gaps.

first match wins 
1. IF Account has a website AND 50+ Google reviews AND 3+ reviews in the last 90 days -> Enters the market. Everything below this floor is excluded, not routed. (LiveSwitch's 2-seat minimum puts entry value near $3.3k/yr. A business doing fewer than a couple of reviewed jobs a month cannot buy it. This floor removes ~55% of raw map output before a single email is written.)
2. IF A competitor virtual-survey or AI-survey tool is detected on the site -> Tier 1 - Already Virtual (Budget exists and the thesis is already sold. The pitch has to be about what their current tool cannot do, never about trying virtual estimates.)
3. IF Otherwise, a live estimator / surveyor / sales consultant job post in the last 45 days -> Tier 2 - Hiring the Fix (An approved budget aimed directly at estimating capacity. They are solving it with headcount instead of software.)
4. IF Otherwise, a field-service platform is detected (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, SmartMoving, Supermove, Movegistics, Chariot) -> Tier 3 - System of Record (They buy operational software and have standardised jobs in one place. The visual half of the job never reaches it.)
5. IF Otherwise, the site advertises free in-home or on-site estimates -> Tier 4 - Truck Roll (A public commitment to visiting every prospect who asks. Pain is present, evidence of budget is not.)
6. IF Otherwise -> Tier 5 - Catch-All (Real job volume and nothing else visible. Copy stays deliberately conservative because volume is all we know.)

Every quote costs drive time, fuel and a paid hour of a skilled person. Most of those quotes do not close, so the unsold-estimate cost is pure margin loss - and it caps how many opportunities a day they can even look at. Every hour in the truck is an hour not quoting.

06 Launch

Four phases over seven weeks

Tier 1 and Tier 3 launch together: a message read and a scale read in the same fortnight.

launch first
01

Weeks 1-2: launch Tier 1 and Tier 3 together. Tier 1 is the smallest list and the most sophisticated audience, so it gives the fastest honest read on whether the message lands. Tier 3 is the volume engine and its seven platform walkthroughs need to be in production early. Running both gives a message read and a scale read in the same fortnight.

02

Week 1 (parallel): start the 45-day job-post scrape immediately, regardless of when Tier 2 launches, so the rolling queue is already replenishing by the time the tier goes live.

03

Weeks 3-4: launch Tier 2 once the Estimator Capacity Calculator exists.

04

Weeks 5-6: launch Tier 4. The largest list, held until sending infrastructure is warmed enough to carry the volume without putting the earlier tiers at risk.

05

Week 7 onward: launch Tier 5 after the 2026 Estimating Benchmark Report is published. Its copy is the broadest, so it benefits most from whatever the first three phases teach us.

06

Throughout: phase-roll sending domains alongside tiers. High-volume tiers never share domains with the tiers proving the message.

07

Testing discipline: two variants maximum per tier. Vary the lead magnet or the research angle in the opening line, and hold subject line, structure and CTA constant so any difference is attributable to one thing. Tier 5 is the exception and opens with a chaos test of two structurally different emails.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacts Excellent. Scale the tier - expand metros and trade categories before touching the copy.
1 per 300 Good. Leave it running untouched.
1 per 450 to 500 Borderline. Test one variant - change the lead magnet or the research angle, hold everything else constant.
Worse than 1 per 500, or reply rate under 1% after 1,500 sends Failing. Cut the tier and rework it from the trigger up.
Bounce rate above 2% on a sending domain Hygiene guardrail. Pause the domain, not the campaign. No performance conclusion is drawn until this is clean.
Negative-sentiment replies above 1.5% of total replies Hygiene guardrail. Rework the opening line before judging the tier.
