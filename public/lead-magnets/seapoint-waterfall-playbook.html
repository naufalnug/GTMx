Prepared for Seapoint Digital

The Seapoint Digital outbound journey.

One cascade across banking, insurance and e-commerce — routed on the growth plans regulated institutions are legally required to publish before they execute them.

Core insight: Five commodity services and one that isn't. Every agency sells SEO and paid media; almost none can get work through a bank's compliance desk. So this programme doesn't sell marketing to regulated companies — it sells the removal of the bottleneck that stops their marketing from shipping. And because banks and insurers must file their growth plans publicly before executing them, every account tells us its own deadline for free.

The signal

Regulated institutions publish their growth plans before they execute them

A bank cannot merge, charter or open a branch without filing publicly. An insurer cannot sell a product in a state without filing the form and rate publicly, with a forward-dated effective date printed on it. Both file quarterly financials showing whether they are actually growing. All of it is free, machine-readable, date-stamped, and available months before the marketing for it exists.

strongest signal
01

It is a legal obligation, not a marketing choice — so it cannot be hidden, delayed or opted out of by the prospect.

02

It carries a forward date. A SERFF effective date or a merger closing timeline tells us when the deadline lands, months in advance.

03

It cuts across all three verticals rather than along them, so one cascade covers banking, insurance and e-commerce without three separate builds.

04

It cannot be bought. No vendor sells 'banks with a 90-day-old merger order and a failing account-opening path' — it is a join across four government datasets and a headless browser scan.

The routing

One cascade, not three vertical campaigns

A community bank and a Shopify Plus brand share no signal ladder, so three verticals would become three independent campaigns that all decay at once. Instead every account is tested against one question, top-down, first match wins: what publicly-filed or publicly-measurable event has put this institution's growth on a clock its current marketing cannot meet?

The tiers

Five states, ordered by how hard the deadline is

Tier 1 is the filed launch with a legal go-live date. Tier 5 catches everything left. Tier 2 deliberately outranks Tier 3 so a funded marketing mandate is never spent on an audit pitch, and Tier 4 sits below Tier 3 because e-commerce lacks the regulatory moat — strong proof, weaker structural fit.

Tier 1: The Filed Launch

A public regulatory filing in the last 90 days carrying a forward effective date 0-120 days out: a merger, charter or branch approval order, or an approved SERFF product filing.

Pain
There is a legally-defined go-live date and no go-to-market behind it. A merger means a rebrand, a deposit-retention programme, two websites that have to become one, and every communication through legal — all on a closing clock. An approved product filing means a live SKU with no landing page, no keyword footprint and no paid search.
Offer
Launch Window Brief — a dated go-live map: the search demand already live for the merged brand or filed product, what a comparable institution captured at the same stage, and the compliance-cleared asset list.
Source
Fed / OCC / FDIC approval orders, polled weekly, joined to SERFF Filing Access across the 12 largest filing states.
Reply density
Highest — a hard external deadline the prospect did not choose and cannot move.

03 30% TAM

Tier 3: The Public Liability

Not Tiers 1-2, and the public site fails a measurable standard: three or more critical or serious WCAG 2.2 AA violations on conversion paths, mobile LCP above 2.5s, INP above 200ms, or two or more YMYL authority gaps.

Pain
For a bank or an insurer these are not cosmetic. Over 4,000 web accessibility lawsuits were filed last year with financial institutions among the most-targeted sectors, and Google holds YMYL money pages to an authority standard their content does not meet. Critically: a marketing director cannot get budget for 'SEO'. They can get budget for a documented exposure.
Offer
Regulated Site Exposure Report — the actual scan, ranked by severity, with the specific WCAG success criterion cited against every failure. The routing pipeline generates it automatically.
Source
axe-core headless scan across five conversion-path URLs, joined to the Chrome UX Report API and a YMYL authority check on money pages.
Reply density
Solid — the volume engine, and the tier that builds the pipeline every other tier reuses.
04 12% TAM

Reply density
Good — a dated vendor deadline, carrying the strongest published proof Seapoint owns.

Tier 5: Catch-all: The Flat Line

Everything remaining in the TAM. Never skipped — the catch-all is what makes this a waterfall rather than a set of segments.

Pain
Growth is flat and it is visible in the institution's own regulatory filings. Whatever marketing is running is not moving the number, and nobody has joined spend to the deposit, member or revenue line.
Offer
Growth Attribution Snapshot — their own FDIC or NCUA filed growth line next to peer institutions in the same county, and an honest read on what marketing can and cannot be credited with.
Source
FDIC Summary of Deposits by county (published annually on 30 June) and NCUA 5300 quarterly membership and share growth.
Reply density
Lowest per contact, largest by volume — launch last, once the sharp tiers are proven.

Focused Focused

YMYL Authority Gap

Most regulated institutions publish rate and product content with no named author, no credentials and no review date — because compliance wrote it and nobody wanted their name on it. That is precisely the content Google holds to the strictest authority standard, which is why these pages plateau.

Filter
Two or more authority gaps on money pages: no author byline, no stated credentials, no review date, or thin content on rate and product pages.
AI strategy
Crawl the institution's rate, product and advice pages and score them against the authority signals Google applies to Your Money or Your Life content. Banking and insurance content sits squarely inside YMYL, so the bar is materially higher than the rest of the site.
Value
Rank where the standard is highest
