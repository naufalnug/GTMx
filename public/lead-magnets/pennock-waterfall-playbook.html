Active-ad status, live creative count, earliest run date, placements and formats. Note: it does NOT publish spend for commercial advertisers — creative count is an intensity proxy only, and no email asserts a prospect's budget.

02

Storefront page-source scrape (1 GET per domain)

Shopify vs Shopify Plus, affiliate network tag (Impact, ShareASale, Awin, Rakuten, CJ, ShopMy, Shopify Collabs), affiliate/ambassador route, retention stack, review platform, product schema JSON-LD.

03

Retail trade press + retailer new-brand indexes

Named, dated brand-to-retailer launch events from BeautyMatter Retail Watch, WWD roundups, and the Sephora, Ulta, Target, Credo, Bluemercury, Nordstrom, Amazon Premium Beauty and Walmart/Wayfair new-arrival indexes.

04

Public job boards

Live requisitions for Growth, Performance, Ecommerce and Retention Marketing roles from LinkedIn Jobs, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and brand careers pages.

05

Answer-engine citation sweep

A fixed 20-prompt category basket run against ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews — citation count, the competitor set cited instead, and share of voice. This is Pennock's own AEO service run at list-build time, which is why the output doubles as the lead magnet.

06

Market seed lists

Shopify and Shopify Plus tech directories filtered to beauty, personal care, apparel and home; retailer brand indexes; Amazon Premium Beauty; TikTok Shop seller directory.

Routing

Exclusive, top-down, 100% coverage

Every brand is tested against the tiers in order and lands in the first one it matches. The catch-all guarantees no qualified brand is left unaddressed.

first match wins 
1. IF Retail launch within the last 120 days, or an announced upcoming launch -> Tier 1 — Retail Launch On The Clock (A dated event with a hard commercial deadline attached. Nothing else in the ladder has a clock.)
2. IF NOT Tier 1 AND active Meta ads AND an affiliate network tag or affiliate page AND no in-house affiliate manager -> Tier 2 — Affiliate Program, No Driver (Proven budget plus a channel they already paid to switch on and demonstrably nobody owns. Inverting a LinkedIn job title is reliable; inverting a tech detection would not be.)
3. IF NOT Tier 1-2 AND a live requisition for Growth, Performance, Ecommerce or Retention Marketing -> Tier 3 — Pricing The Gap At A Salary (They have already admitted the gap and put a salary number on it.)
4. IF NOT Tier 1-3 AND active Meta ads AND two or fewer citations across the 20-prompt category basket -> Tier 4 — Renting 100% Of Discovery (Proven budget, zero owned discovery. A positive measurement, not an absence inference — you run the prompts and record what came back.)
5. IF Everything else in the qualified market -> Tier 5 — Catch-All: Channel Concentration (Guarantees 100% coverage. No qualified brand is left unaddressed.)
