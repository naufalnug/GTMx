Signal

The signal is where they sit in the practice ladder

LinkedIn Sales Nav / Apollo already names firm owners, independent practitioners, employed staff accountants/bookkeepers/tax pros, or none of those. That practice_stage is stronger than a specialty guess and cheap to collect in one pull.

strongest signal
01

Apollo + LinkedIn Sales Nav filtered to Universal's commercial TAM (US bookkeepers, accountants, tax preparers, payroll specialists, aspiring practice owners) is the spine Roger already sells into. Public TAM account count is not published on universalaccounting.com; treat size as estimated (see universal-accounting-magnet-flags.txt) and keep each launch tier ≥~1,500 contacts where possible.

02

LinkedIn title + headline + company type returns practice_stage in one pull: Owner / Founder / Principal / Managing Partner / Firm Owner of a bookkeeping, accounting, or tax practice for Tier 1; Independent / Self-employed / Freelance / Solo bookkeeper, EA, tax preparer for Tier 2; Staff Accountant / Bookkeeper / Tax Preparer / Payroll Specialist (W-2 employer) for Tier 3 — enough to route without a second expensive enrichment product.

03

The ladder maps to live Universal offers on universalaccounting.com: Profit & Growth Expert + Advanced Coaching + GrowCon for firm owners; Virtual Bookkeeper's Roadmap + start-up certs + coaching for independents; Professional Bookkeeper / Tax Preparer / QuickBooks Specialist for Better-Job W-2 pros; soft Success Journey + free Accountrepreneurs Challenge for remaining TAM — plus since-1979 tenure and tens-of-thousands trained as proof language.

04

Specialty (bookkeeping vs tax vs payroll) and city are scrape filters and merge tags, not a second waterfall. Apollo is email last after the account is routed.

Waterfall

One cascade routes every in-TAM accounting professional

First match wins. Firm owner/principal first, then independent solo practitioner, then employed staff seeking a better job, then every remaining in-TAM accounting-interested lead.

first match wins 
1. IF in_tam AND firm_owner_detected = true (LinkedIn title/headline names Owner, Founder, Principal, Managing Partner, President, or Firm Owner of a bookkeeping, accounting, tax, or payroll practice) AND not already_student -> Tier 1 - Premier Firm Growth Brief (They already run a practice and feel the ceiling on compliance-only work. Strongest intent for Universal's Build Your Business path — Profit & Growth Expert (169+ lessons, Profit First + Value Builder modules), Advanced Coaching with Roger Knecht, and GrowCon community. Pain inferred from trigger: owners stuck in production instead of advisory/marketing.)
2. IF in_tam AND independent_practice_detected = true (Self-employed, Independent, Freelance, Solo bookkeeper / tax preparer / EA / accountant; company type = self-employed) AND no Tier-1 firm-owner match AND not already_student -> Tier 2 - Practice Launch Roadmap (They hung out a shingle but still need turnkey workflow, pricing, and client acquisition. Maps to Virtual Bookkeeper's Roadmap + Professional Bookkeeper / Tax Preparer certs + marketing coaching — Start Your Business path on the homepage.)
3. IF in_tam AND employed_staff_detected = true (Staff Accountant, Bookkeeper, Tax Preparer, Payroll Specialist, Accounting Clerk at an employer; W-2 framing) AND no Tier 1–2 practice match AND not already_student -> Tier 3 - Better-Job Certification Brief (They want competence credentials and a pay bump without years of school. Maps to Professional Bookkeeper™, Professional Tax Preparer™, QuickBooks™ Specialist, Color Accounting™ — Better Job path (as little as four weeks / 3–6 weeks on homepage).)
4. IF in_tam AND none of the above -> Catch-all - Success Journey / Accountrepreneurs Challenge (Still in TAM (career changers, aspiring accountants, adjacent finance roles considering bookkeeping/tax). Soft CTA around free Accountrepreneurs Challenge (90-day) and Success Journey overview. Mandatory 100% coverage.)
