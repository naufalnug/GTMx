Prepared for Bubba's LA

The Bubba's LA outbound journey.

One waterfall that finds the moment a hero visual gets commissioned, before the brief goes out.

Core insight: Nobody wakes up wanting a $40k animation. They wake up with a date, and a hero visual that doesn't exist yet. Both of Bubba's best-fit verticals are legally required to publish dated filings before anything ships, so that date is public months before the brief is written.

01

The signal nobody has packaged

Four public, dated, legally-mandated filings tell you a hero visual is about to be commissioned. No vendor sells this list, because it doesn't exist as a product.

strongest signal
01

Every US alcoholic beverage needs a public label approval before it ships. The registry is free, needs no login, is searchable by date, and publishes the approved artwork about 48 hours after clearance.

02

A fresh approval means the packaging is final, the ship date is locked, and no motion asset exists yet. That is the highest-intent moment in the entire food and beverage calendar.

03

It hands over the personalisation too. We can reference the actual approved label, by product name, before the brand's own agency has finished the deck.

04

None of it can be bought. There is no data vendor selling label approvals as a creative-services prospect list, so no competing studio is working the same window.

TTB Public COLA Registry

The alcohol and beverage market, the new-product trigger, the approval date, and the approved label artwork. Free, no registration, searchable by date range.

USPTO Trademark Search

Packaged goods and non-alcoholic food and beverage. Section 1(b) intent-to-use filings mean the product does not exist yet but is coming.

Meta Ad Library

Whether they are spending, how old the oldest running creative is, and whether the set is motion or flat photography. Free, no login.

California Film Commission

The approved-projects list from a $3.75bn, five-year tax credit programme. Names the production company, the project, and the allocation.

Trade show new-product showcases

Expo West, Summer Fancy Food, NACS, Bar Convent Brooklyn. A dated launch calendar with the brands attached.

06

Job posts and agency press pages

Producer and 3D artist requisitions, plus publicly announced new business wins. Both mean production volume just outran the team.

02

Gate first, then route

A $25k floor means most of the world is not a prospect. Filter the market before routing it, then test every survivor against one ladder, top to bottom.

first match wins 
1. IF The account fails the qualification gate: under 25 staff brand-side, no distribution, no funding, no live ads, or it is another studio or production company -> Discard. Do not send. (A $25k floor means routing accounts who cannot fund it burns sends and drags every tier's numbers down. Rival studios are peers, not prospects.)
2. IF A label approval cleared in the last 180 days at a producer with prior approval history, OR an intent-to-use trademark was filed in the last 270 days at 15+ headcount, OR they appeared in a trade show new-product showcase in the last 120 days -> Tier 1, Launch Filed No Film (The artwork is locked and the hero film is not. This is a legally-filed, dated declaration that a product is coming and its visuals do not exist yet.)
3. IF NOT Tier 1, AND there are 6 or more active ads, AND the oldest running creative is 90+ days old, AND motion is under 25% of the set or no CGI is detectable -> Tier 2, Paying to Distribute Stale Creative (The media budget is already committed. The creative is the bottleneck, not the spend, and refresh currently means recolouring the same photograph.)
4. IF NOT Tier 1 or 2, AND a producer or 3D requisition opened in the last 90 days, OR a new business win was announced in the last 90 days, OR three or more creative requisitions are open at once -> Tier 3, Volume Outran the Team (Headcount is a 90-day fix for a two-week problem. Overflow is going to whoever answers first with a reel that matches the account.)
5. IF Everything else that cleared the gate -> Tier 4, the catch-all (No dated trigger fired, but their own site shows a static-dominant visual mix. Skipping this tier would leave 56% of the qualified market unrouted.)

03

Four tiers, one question

How close is this account to commissioning a hero visual? Tier 1 is a legally-filed answer. Tier 4 is an educated read. Nothing is left unrouted.

click each card
01 ~8% TAM

Tier 1: Launch Filed, No Film

A label approval cleared in the last 180 days at a producer with prior filing history, an intent-to-use trademark filed in the last 270 days by a company with 15+ staff, or a named appearance in a trade show new-product showcase in the last 120 days.

Pain
The packaging artwork is final and the ship date is locked, but no motion asset exists. Retail point-of-sale, Amazon A+ pages, paid social and the launch film all need hero product visuals, and the existing flat photography cannot carry a launch.
Offer
Launch Visual Gap Report: their approved label, three hero frames built from it, and the map of six assets one shoot day would deliver
Source
TTB Public COLA Registry, USPTO intent-to-use filings, trade show new-product showcases
Reply density
Highest in the cascade. Smallest list, dated intent, and the personalisation writes itself off the filing.
02 ~22% TAM

Tier 2: Paying to Distribute Stale Creative

Six or more active ads, the oldest still running for 90 days or more, and either under 25 percent motion or no CGI product work detectable anywhere in the set.

Pain
Media budget is already committed. Static flat-lay in a motion-first feed means decaying click-through and climbing cost per acquisition, and by the time it is obvious they are eight weeks from having anything new to swap in.
Offer
Creative Fatigue Teardown: a grid of their live ads with days-live per creative, motion share, the three placements bleeding hardest, and the variants we would cut to replace them
Source
Meta Ad Library sweep, free and no login required
Reply density
Middle of the pack, but the largest addressable volume with a genuine signal behind it.
03 ~14% TAM

Tier 3: Volume Outran the Team

An open producer or 3D requisition in the last 90 days, a publicly announced new business win in the last 90 days, or three or more creative requisitions open simultaneously.

Pain
The work landed before the team did. Between now and a hire starting, the executive producer is handing overflow to whoever answers fastest with a reel that matches the account.
Offer
Overflow Capacity One-Pager: live availability window, day-rate bands, the motion-control rig spec, and three reels matched to the accounts they actually service
Source
Job posts, agency careers pages, agency press pages and company posts
Reply density
Best lifetime economics in the document. An agency that buys once buys four more times a year.
04 ~56% TAM

Tier 4: Catch-all

Everything else that clears the qualification gate. This tier is not optional; without it the waterfall leaves more than half its market unrouted.

Pain
No dated trigger fired, but their own site and social show a static-dominant visual mix. The product is the hero of the category and the visuals treat it like a stock photograph.
Offer
The 60-Second Product Test: send one product, get back a single motion frame concept and a shot list. No charge and no call.
Source
The gated market minus every tier above it, plus a visual-mix read off their own site
Reply density
Lowest per contact, highest absolute volume. Softest possible ask to compensate.

Label Approval, 30 Days Fresh

The sharpest version of Tier 1. A 30-day window means the artwork is signed off and the creative conversation is happening internally right now, but the vendor has almost certainly not been chosen.

Filter
Label approval cleared in the last 30 days, producer has 3+ prior approvals
AI strategy
Pull the daily approval feed, read the fanciful name and the approved artwork, and open on a specific visual detail of the label. The email references a document the prospect knows is real and assumes nobody else has read.
Value
Arrive before the brief
Focused Focused

Intent-to-Use, Nine Months Out

Lower urgency than a fresh approval but a longer runway, which suits a studio that needs lead time. Also reaches the packaged-goods brands a beverage-only signal would miss entirely.

Filter
Section 1(b) trademark filed in food, staples or beverage classes in the last 270 days, applicant has 15+ staff
AI strategy
Catch non-alcoholic launches, which file no label approval. Earlier in the cycle than a COLA, so the copy shifts from 'the film isn't booked' to 'when you brief this out'.
Value
Get on the list early
Niche Niche

A seasonal spike rather than an always-on feed. Worth timing the send to land two to three weeks after the show closes, when the follow-up scramble starts.

Filter
Named in an Expo West, Summer Fancy Food, NACS or Bar Convent Brooklyn new-product listing in the last 120 days
AI strategy
The showcase entry is a public commitment to a launch with a date attached. Reference the show by name; it proves a human read the list rather than scraped a database.
Value
Arrive before the brief

Oldest active creative 90+ days live, six or more ads running

Library, Not Asset

The highest-leverage reframe in the document for performance buyers, because it converts a project cost into a cost-per-asset they can defend to finance.

Filter
Ecommerce and performance-led brands with heavy paid social
AI strategy
Reframe the purchase entirely. They think they are buying one film for $40k; the pitch is that they are buying twenty-plus variants off a single build, which changes the unit maths.

Save money

The cleanest agency-side trigger there is. Job posts are reliably detectable, unlike tech stack absence, so this tier does not depend on inference.

Short window and high relevance. Send inside three weeks of the announcement or the moment has passed and the resourcing decision is already made.

Publicly announced client win in the last 90 days
AI strategy
Congratulate, then immediately name the consequence: new account, new volume, existing team. The congratulation earns the second sentence.

One Spot, Fixed Scope

The follow-up angle that converts hesitation into a trial. One spot, one scope, fixed price. Lose one job or gain a bench.

Filter
Agencies that opened but did not reply to the capacity pitch
AI strategy
De-risk explicitly. Producers hesitate on a new studio mid-flight because the client is live and there is no room to be wrong. Shrink the first commitment until the risk is trivial.
Value
Reduce risk
Broad Broad

The 60-Second Product Test

The softest CTA in the playbook, doing the most work. With 56% of the market here, the conversion mechanism has to be a free artefact rather than a calendar link.

Filter
Any gated account that fired no trigger
AI strategy
Do not sell. Ask for one product name and return a free motion frame concept and a shot list. The ask is smaller than a meeting, so it clears the bar for people with no active need.
Value
See it first
Broad Broad

Photogenic and Still

Works because it is true and specific to their brand. Requires a real look at the site, which is what separates this from every other studio email in the inbox.

Filter
Static-dominant visual mix on their own site and social
AI strategy
Observe rather than diagnose. Note that the product is genuinely photogenic and that almost everything showing it is a still. It reads as taste, not as criticism.
Value
Make money
Niche Niche

CGI-Only, No Shipping

A one-field switch applied at copy assembly. Motion-control needs the product in Burbank; CGI does not, so geography changes the offer rather than the routing.

Filter
International accounts, or products that cannot practically ship to Burbank
AI strategy
Switch the offer, not the tier. Drop tabletop entirely and pitch a pure CGI product build from label art and dielines, which has no geographic constraint.
Value
Remove a blocker

Always on, low volume, Los Angeles only. Depends entirely on the rig being owned in-house rather than accessed through a partner, which is an open question.

06

Thirteen weeks, phased

Warm the infrastructure while reactivation runs by hand. Tier 1 proves the thesis. Tier 3 compounds. Tier 2 scales. Tier 4 runs underneath forever.

launch first
01

Weeks 0 to 3. Warm four to six sending domains with three mailboxes each, ramping to 25 to 30 sends per mailbox per day. Never send from bubbas.la itself. Open tracking stays off.

02

Weeks 0 to 3, in parallel. Run the alumni and job-change reactivation by hand from a real inbox. Zero deliverability risk, and the most likely source of revenue in month one.

03

Weeks 0 to 3, in parallel. Close the two launch blockers: one real outcome metric from one past project, and a yes or no on whether the motion-control rig is owned in-house.

04

Weeks 3 to 6. Tier 1 only, around 600 contacts per week. Smallest and sharpest list, proves the thesis fastest, and produces the case study every other tier needs.

05

Weeks 6 to 9. Add Tier 3. Agencies are repeat buyers, so one win compounds into several jobs a year. Better lifetime economics than Tier 2 despite a list a third of the size.

06

Weeks 9 to 13. Add Tier 2, but only once the Creative Fatigue Teardown is productised down to roughly twenty minutes of analyst time per account. At an hour each the unit economics fail across nine thousand accounts.

07

Week 13 onward. Tier 4 at full volume underneath everything else, and layer the entertainment side campaign onto the next Film Commission allocation window.

08

Ongoing. A daily label-approval pull and a weekly ad-library sweep. That is the entire maintenance cost of the cascade.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacted Excellent. Scale this tier hard and feed it more list.
1 per 300 Good. Leave it alone and let it run untouched.
1 per 450 to 500 Borderline. Test one copy variant, changing the lead magnet or the notice-line angle only. Hold everything else constant.
Worse than 1 per 500, or under 1% reply rate after 1,500 sends Failing. Cut the tier and rework the trigger, not the copy.
Median first project under $25,000 The qualification gate is too loose. Tighten the distribution, funding and headcount filters before adding volume.
