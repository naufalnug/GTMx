Your D-ID waterfall journey.

A scrollable GTMx strategy artifact for routing marketing, L&D, CX, and sales-enablement buyers into the right D-ID Video Studio, Visual AI Agent, or competitor-switch offer. List building, campaign angles, and sample copy, prepared for Nader Wahba, VP Sales, North America.

Core insight: Nader sells one D-ID digital-human platform — Video Studio + Visual AI Agents + AI Avatars — not a different product per vertical. Marketing vs L&D vs CX vs sales enablement is a merge tag after routing, not a second waterfall. The single routing signal is ai_avatar_maturity: what a lead source can already see about how they produce talking-head or interactive experiences — competing AI avatar/video platform, heavy filmed video production without an AI avatar tool, conversational AI / chatbot without a face, or none. That ladder maps to D-ID's live offers: competitor switch into V4 real-time agents + Studio, replace costly video production with Studio avatars, overlay a Visual AI Agent on existing LLM/chat, then a soft remaining-TAM Visual Agents playbook.

Waterfall

One cascade routes every in-TAM marketing, L&D, CX, and sales account

First match wins. Competitor AI avatar platform first, then filmed-production-without-AI-avatar, then chatbot-without-visual-agent, then every remaining in-TAM buyer.

first match wins 
1. IF in_tam AND ai_avatar_maturity = competitor_platform (HeyGen, Synthesia, Colossyan, Hour One, Elai, Tavus, or adjacent AI avatar/talking-head video tool) AND not already_customer -> Tier 1 - Competitor Switch + Real-Time Agents Brief (They already budget an AI avatar / talking-head stack. Strongest switch intent for D-ID as the real-time Visual Agents + Studio platform (V4 latency, API, 120+ languages) versus pre-recorded-first competitors.)
2. IF in_tam AND ai_avatar_maturity = filmed_production_no_avatar (video producer / instructional designer / studio roles OR human-presenter training/marketing video library) AND no Tier-1 competitor_platform AND not already_customer -> Tier 2 - Video Studio Replace Production Brief (They still shoot or hire for talking-head content at scale. Maps to D-ID Video Studio: minutes-not-days avatar videos from scripts/decks, brand-adaptable, multilingual — without inventing an AI tool they do not have.)
3. IF in_tam AND ai_avatar_maturity = chat_no_visual_agent (Intercom / Drift / Zendesk chat / custom conversational AI on site or product) AND no Tier 1-2 AND not already_customer -> Tier 3 - Visual AI Agent Overlay Brief (They already run text or voice chat but no face. Maps to D-ID Visual AI Agents: LLM-connected, RAG-grounded, WebRTC-streamed digital humans for CX, website concierge, or training roleplay.)
4. IF in_tam AND none of the above -> Catch-all - D-ID Visual Agents Playbook (Still in TAM (marketing, L&D, CX, sales enablement, developer platforms). Softest CTA around AI video + interactive agents. Mandatory 100% coverage.)
