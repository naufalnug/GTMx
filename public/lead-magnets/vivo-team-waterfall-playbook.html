02

How every account gets routed

First match wins, top to bottom. Every account exits with exactly one tier and the catch-all guarantees nothing is left out.

first match wins 
1. IF An L&D, Talent Development, Organizational Development or Leadership Development requisition is open -> Tier 1 - Mandate & Budget (Budget and mandate already exist. This is the highest-intent state in the entire market.)
2. IF Not Tier 1, and Great Place to Work certified OR an engagement survey or people report is published -> Tier 2 - Measured but Not Moved (Proven willingness to spend on people-program measurement, but sentiment data they cannot act on or price.)
3. IF Not Tier 1 or 2, and three or more front-line manager requisitions are open OR first-time-manager language appears in a requisition -> Tier 3 - Accidental Managers (Structural strain. Leaders are being made faster than they are being prepared, and the turnover that causes opens the next req.)
4. IF Not Tier 1, 2 or 3, and Glassdoor reviews are 25 or more with a Senior Management subscore of 3.4 or lower -> Tier 4 - The Score Everyone Can Already See (The pain is public and quantified, but there is no diagnosis behind it and no dollar figure attached.)
5. IF Anything else in the addressable market -> Tier 5 - Catch-All Benchmark (No visible signal, but every organization with a leadership layer carries unpriced lost productivity. Coverage tier.)

Launch sequence

Tier 1 first for the fastest read on the measurement wedge. Tier 3 second because it reuses the same scrape at zero marginal cost.

launch first
01

Weeks 1 to 2, build. Sending infrastructure with a US and Canadian domain split so a CASL complaint cannot contaminate US deliverability. Mailboxes into a 30-day warmup. ATS, Great Place to Work and Glassdoor scrapers built. The two launch-blocking proof gaps closed: a turnover-reduction figure and a price anchor.

02

Weeks 3 to 6, Tier 1 only. Roughly 3,400 contacts. Highest intent, and the fastest read on whether the measurement wedge lands. Weekly ATS re-scrape live from day one.

03

Weeks 5 to 9, Tier 3. It launches before Tier 2 because it draws on the same job-board scrape as Tier 1 and therefore carries zero marginal build cost.

04

Weeks 8 to 13, Tiers 2 and 4 together. Tier 4 runs two completely different copies head to head rather than a refinement test, because its framing is the one variable that decides the tier.

05

Week 12 onward, Tier 5. The catch-all launches once the four signal tiers have stabilised and the best-performing lead magnet is known.

06

Week 16 onward, side campaigns. New people leader first: a CHRO or VP People 0 to 120 days into the seat needs a defensible baseline before their first exec review, and the Vital Statistics Report is that baseline.

07

Ongoing cadence. ATS scrape weekly because Tier 1 and 3 signals are perishable. Great Place to Work quarterly because certifications are annual. Glassdoor quarterly because subscores move slowly.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacts Excellent. Scale this tier immediately.
1 per 300 Good. Leave it running untouched.
1 per 450 to 500 Borderline. Test one copy variant, changing the lead magnet or the opening research angle only.
Worse than 1 per 500, or reply rate under 1% after 1,500 sends Cut the tier and rework it. Do not nurse it.
Two copy variants per tier, maximum Hold everything else constant. Testing more than one variable at a time produces results nobody can act on.
