Waterfall

One cascade routes every in-TAM mid-market buyer

First match wins. AI/ML hiring first, then software IC hiring without AI roles, then product/digital leadership hiring, then every remaining in-TAM company in Citrusbug's industries.

first match wins 
1. IF in_tam AND ai_role_open = true (AI Engineer, ML Engineer, LLM/GenAI, Data Scientist, MLOps, Computer Vision, NLP) AND not already_customer -> Tier 1 - AI Delivery Partner Brief (They are already staffing AI. Strongest intent for a Citrusbug / Aitiate build partner using published delivery proof (500+ projects, 98% retention, Phelix / Sully / Valene outcomes).)
2. IF in_tam AND eng_ic_role_open = true (Full-Stack, Backend, Frontend, Mobile, Platform, DevOps) AND ai_role_open = false AND not already_customer -> Tier 2 - Dedicated Build / Capacity Brief (They need engineering capacity now. Maps to Citrusbug Solutions + Services (dedicated team / custom platform) without inventing an AI hire they have not posted.)
3. IF in_tam AND leadership_role_open = true (CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Product/Digital/Innovation, Chief Digital Officer) AND no Tier 1-2 match AND not already_customer -> Tier 3 - AI Readiness Assessment (Leadership is hiring to own the roadmap. Maps to Citrusbug Consulting / AI Readiness path — clarity before build.)
4. IF in_tam AND none of the above -> Catch-all - AI Opportunity Playbook (Still in TAM (mid-market buyers in Citrusbug's named industries). Softest CTA around practical AI value and Secure ADLC delivery. Mandatory 100% coverage.)
