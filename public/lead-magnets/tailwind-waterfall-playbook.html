Your Tailwind waterfall journey.

One priority-routed cascade over ~246,000 accounts, routed on data Pinterest publishes for free.

That single read replaces an enrichment budget, and it produces the personalisation and the lead magnet in the same act.

01 / Signal

The gap Pinterest publishes for free

Three unauthenticated reads, joined, give you the market and the routing signal in one pull. No API key, no login, no enrichment vendor in the critical path.

strongest signal
01

Domain verification status is public and is proof of past commercial intent. They put the tag on the site, then stopped. You can name the exact date.

02

Pinterest search is a free competitive oracle. Query the term a brand sells under and see whether they own it or a competitor does.

03

Display-ad stacks publish minimum-session thresholds, so detecting the script proves a traffic floor without buying a single traffic-estimation credit.

04

It is behavioural, not firmographic, so no enrichment vendor sells it, and it changes every week rather than every quarter.

02 / Routing

One cascade, first match wins

Every account is tested top to bottom and lands in exactly one campaign. The top of the waterfall is set by revenue per account, not signal strength, because pricing is per Pinterest account.

first match wins 
1. IF Existing Tailwind user, Canada, Germany, Austria, EU sole traders, UK non-registered entities, or under 50 followers and 25 Pins -> SUPPRESS before routing (Existing users are a warm database, not cold outbound. CASL and UWG section 7 bar the excluded geographies outright, and PECR treats sole traders as individuals, which matters because most UK creators and Pinterest VAs are sole traders.)
2. IF Sells Pinterest management as a service, or runs three or more Pinterest profiles -> TIER 1 - Portfolio Operator (Pricing is per Pinterest account, so one operator carries 10 to 40 seats plus a recurring affiliate line. Revenue per account, not signal strength, sets the top of this waterfall.)
3. IF Domain verified on Pinterest AND no Pin in more than 90 days -> TIER 2 - Claimed & Stalled (Verification is work. They did it, which proves the intent was real, and then they stopped. Something organisational ended it, not a strategic decision.)
4. IF Pinned within 90 days AND absent from the top 20 for their own head keyword AND a named competitor ranks -> TIER 3 - Active but Outranked (They are doing the work and still losing the result. That is not an effort problem, it is a keyword problem, which is exactly what the product fixes.)
5. IF Display-ad stack detected, or 50,000+ monthly sessions, with a thin Pinterest surface -> TIER 4 - Traffic-Rich, Pinterest-Thin (The ad script proves a revenue-relevant traffic floor for free, and identifies publishers whose revenue is a direct function of sessions.)
6. IF Everything else in a Pinterest-native vertical -> TIER 5 - Pinterest-Native, Pinterest-Absent (catch-all) (Nobody is left behind, but this is the tier that stops paying for itself first, so it runs at capped volume and is the first cut when spend is constrained.)

06 / Launch

Tier 2 first, deliberately

The default says launch the hottest tier first. Here Tier 2 goes first: largest high-signal list, no search-join engineering, offer deliverable on day one.

launch first
01

Weeks 0 to 2. Build infrastructure: 23 sending domains, 90 mailboxes, full warmup. Never send from tailwindapp.com, because it carries high-volume transactional mail and reputation damage there is a product incident, not a marketing setback.

02

Weeks 0 to 2. Build the Pinterest profile-scrape pipeline and template report fulfilment, so every offer is deliverable inside 24 hours. Suppress the existing free-user database before a single send.

03

Week 3. Launch Tier 2, Claimed & Stalled. Sharpest personalisation, no new engineering, offer deliverable on day one. This is the deliberate deviation from launching the hottest tier first.

04

Week 4. Add Tier 4, Traffic-Rich. Ad-stack detection is a one-line page-source check, which makes it the largest cheap-to-qualify list in the programme.

05

Week 6. Add Tier 3, Active but Outranked, once the Pinterest search join is automated. Build that join during weeks 3 to 5 while Tier 2 is already running.

06

Week 7. Add Tier 1, Portfolio Operator, once the partner and affiliate motion is decided. Highest value per yes, so it is worth waiting to do properly rather than launching it early and badly.

07

Week 9. Add Tier 5, the catch-all, running both chaos-test variants at capped volume. Roughly 50% of send capacity should sit on Tiers 1 and 2, 30% on Tiers 3 and 4, and no more than 20% here.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacts Perfect. Scale the tier.
1 per 300 Good. Let it run.
1 per 450 to 500 Borderline. Test one copy variant, holding everything else constant.
Worse than 1 per 500, or under 1% reply after 1,500+ sends Cut the tier and rework it.
Spam complaints above 0.08% Stop the tier immediately. This audience is tool-literate and reports aggressively.
Cost per activated free account above blended paid CAC The real scorecard. At $17.99 to $29.98 a month this programme is a paid-acquisition channel, not a meetings channel.
