Waterfall

One cascade routes every US and Canadian brokerage office

First match wins. Paper flyers still taped in the window go first, then brand-new or renovating offices, then multi-office groups with uneven digital coverage, then every remaining in-TAM storefront Maps still lists.

first match wins 
1. IF already_customer = true -> Exclude (Already a WindoVision / TouchPoint customer office. A franchise brand in the gallery is not enough to exclude.)
2. IF in_tam AND paper_flyers = true (Street View or listing photos still show paper listing flyers taped in the window) AND not already_customer -> Tier 1 - Paper-to-Pixel Window Brief (They are still running the paper window WindoVision was built to replace. Strongest intent. Homepage and About already contrast taped listings with live stock; Brad Whittaker's published quote names walk-ins frustrated that paper flyers were already pending or sold.)
3. IF in_tam AND renovation_or_opening = true (new office, remodel, relocation, or grand opening on Maps, site, or local news) AND no Tier-1 paper-flyer match AND not already_customer -> Tier 2 - Fit-Out Storefront Kit (They are already spending on the physical office. Maps to WindoVision as the 24/7 listing and lead layer in the fit-out — dual thru-glass, MLS/IDX, QR, exterior audio — before the window defaults back to paper.)
4. IF in_tam AND multi_office_gap = true (same brand / group shows 2+ Maps listings and digital storefront coverage is uneven) AND no Tier 1-2 match AND not already_customer -> Tier 3 - Portfolio Coverage Map (The group already bought a premium location somewhere and left other windows analog. A coverage map is the first offer: which offices still leak after-hours traffic, and where preferred-partner slots can offset cost.)
5. IF in_tam AND none of the above -> Catch-all - 24/7 Lead Window Playbook (Still in TAM (US/Canada brick-and-mortar real estate offices Maps already lists). Soft CTA around after-hours email and text leads, live MLS/IDX stock, and a first conversation. Mandatory 100% coverage.)
