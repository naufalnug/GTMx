Signal

The signal is the public ad stack

A publisher's ads.txt file and detected ad tech show who already takes a cut of their inventory. That is stronger than a traffic guess, and it is free to collect.

01

02

BuiltWith and Wappalyzer confirm the same networks plus AdSense, Ad Manager, and Prebid when ads.txt is thin.

03

The signal maps to live Ezoic copy: keep the domain and the audience, put every impression into an auction, and use first-party identity instead of a walled garden.

Waterfall

One cascade routes every publisher

First match wins. Premium competitor networks go first, then AdSense and header bidding, then first-party or video inventory, then everyone else.

first match wins 
1. IF already_on_ezoic = true -> Exclude (Already a customer)
2. IF premium_network in {Mediavine, Raptive, AdThrive, Freestar} -> Tier 1 (Premium network switch)
3. IF adsense_or_gam = true OR header_bidding = true -> Tier 2 (Independent stack lift)
4. IF first_party_signal != none OR video_player_detected = true -> Tier 3 (First-party and video)
5. IF remaining independent publisher or video platform in TAM -> Catch-all (Not monetizing yet)

Tiers

Each stack gets a different first offer

Switchers get a network teardown. AdSense sites get a lift brief. List- and video-led sites get a first-party or UX note. The rest get a no-upfront-cost starter.

Scale / cut rules

Dense positive replies on the first pass Scale that tier. Keep the winning revenue or engagement variant.
Steady replies, no clear winner yet Let it run. Do not mix advertiser-demand copy into this TAM.
Thin replies after a full first pass Swap only the lead magnet or the first-line stack mention. Do not change the route.
No density after a full pass of the tier Cut that copy variant. Keep the exclusive waterfall.
