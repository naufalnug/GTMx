Your CleanCloud waterfall journey.

A scrollable GTMx strategy artifact for routing laundromats, dry cleaners, alterations, and shoe-repair shops into the right CleanCloud POS, pickup-and-delivery, multi-store, or starter offer. List building, campaign angles, and sample copy, prepared for Ricard Pryce.

Core insight: Ricard already sells one laundry operating system so shops stop running tickets, routes, and customer comms on pen-and-paper, a Windows dry-clean POS, or a laundromat-only stack that does not cover garment care plus pickup-and-delivery in one login. The one list is local laundry services only: Google Maps laundromats, dry cleaners, alterations, and shoe-repair shops in the English-speaking markets CleanCloud already sells into — not restaurants, not generic retail POS. The one routing signal is maps_ops_state: how the shop already shows up on Maps, because a Places scrape already returns a competitor POS named in reviews or on the site, pickup-and-delivery attributes on the listing, same-brand location count, or none of those, in one pull. Category (laundromat vs dry cleaner vs alterations) is a merge tag after routing, not a second waterfall.

Waterfall

One cascade routes every in-TAM laundry shop

First match wins. Named competitor POS in reviews or on-site goes first, then listings that already advertise pickup and delivery, then same-brand multi-site operators, then every remaining Maps laundromat, dry cleaner, alterations, or shoe-repair shop.
