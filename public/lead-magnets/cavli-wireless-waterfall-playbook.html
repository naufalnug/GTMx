Prepared for Cavli Wireless

The Cavli Wireless outbound journey.

One waterfall built on the one database that already tells you which competitor's module is on their board.

Core insight: Every cellular device sold in the US must be certified by the FCC, and the filing is published in full - including the label exhibit that reads "Contains FCC ID: XMR...". XMR is Quectel. RI7 is Telit. N7N is Sierra Wireless. XPY is u-blox. One free query returns the OEM, the named contact, and which competitor's module is designed into their product. It is the bill of materials of the entire US connected-device industry, published by the US government, and no enrichment vendor sells it.

The signal nobody else is reading

Module attribution from federal certification filings. Free, weekly-refreshed, and impossible to buy from a list vendor.

strongest signal

Modular approval rules force every host device to print the module's own FCC ID on its label and in its manual - so the competitor's part number is published, by law, in a free federal database.

02

How every account gets routed

Six conditions tested top to bottom. First match wins. No account in the market is left unrouted.

first match wins 
1. IF Module vendor is Chinese AND (regulated end market OR federal contractor) -> Tier 1 - Compliance Deadline (Section 5949 applies to them by statute on 23 December 2027, and a module change is a 12-18 month respin and recert programme. They are already behind.)
2. IF Module vendor is Chinese (commercial end market) -> Tier 2 - Commercial De-risk (A detected sole module is single-source by definition, on a vendor under active US government scrutiny.)
3. IF Part number matches a published end-of-life notice, OR grantee code is XPY (u-blox) -> Tier 3 - Forced Migration (Their supplier announced the exit publicly and there is a last-time-buy date. Doing nothing means the product stops shipping.)
4. IF Radio generation is 2G-only or 3G, OR all certified bands sit on retirement schedules -> Tier 4 - Sunset Stranded (Carriers published the sunset dates. The deployed fleet goes dark and the respin cost is already committed.)
5. IF First cellular certification within 18 months, OR hiring a cellular / embedded firmware role -> Tier 5 - First Design Cycle (A first cellular certification means a first-time integration. They have just discovered it is four vendors and nine months, not one part number.)
6. IF Anything else with a live cellular certification -> Tier 6 - Catch-all, Second Source (Every hardware team carries single-source component risk and almost none have a validated alternate qualified.)

Section 5949 Countdown

The only tier where the prospect has a statutory obligation with a date on it. Copy states the statute and the date as facts and never editorialises about China - many of these manufacturers have partners there, and the moment it reads as a political pitch it is dead.

Filter
Chinese-grantee module in the filing AND (SAM.gov federal contractor match OR regulated end market: government, defence, utility, medical, critical infrastructure)
AI strategy
Parse the label exhibit and manual PDF for the "Contains FCC ID" string, resolve the grantee code to the vendor, then cross-match the applicant against federal contracting records. The first line names their product, their filing number and their exact module part number.
Value
Remove a dated legal risk

Launch plan and thresholds

Dated tiers first. Data build in weeks 1-3, first replies by week 5.

launch first
01

Weeks 1-3, data build. Ingest the FCC bulk authorisation data, run document full-text matching for "Contains FCC ID" strings, attribute grantee codes to module vendors, and produce the true market count and tier distribution. Build the swap-map cross-reference for the top 40 competitor part numbers.

02

Weeks 4-7, launch Tiers 1 and 3. The two dated-deadline tiers go first: smallest volume, highest conviction, fastest proof. Target 1,500 or more contacts each before judging either.

03

Weeks 8-11, launch Tier 2. The largest high-intent tier, and it reuses Tier 1's swap map, so incremental build cost is near zero.

04

Weeks 12-15, launch Tiers 4 and 5. Requires the sunset exposure generator and the connectivity cost model to be built first.

05

Week 16 onward, launch Tier 6 at volume, once the five tiers above are calibrated and evaluation kit fulfilment is proven.

06

Ongoing: weekly certification data refresh re-scores the market automatically. New filings enter the top of the waterfall each week, and accounts re-route themselves upward as they certify new products.

07

Before launch, close the proof gaps: Cavli has no public named case study or quantified migration outcome. One named migration customer carries Tiers 1, 2 and 3. Do not send copy with unfilled brackets - cut the sentence rather than soften it.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacts Excellent. Scale the tier and expand the source.
1 per 300 Good. Let it run untouched.
1 per 450-500 Borderline. Test one copy variant and hold everything else constant.
Worse than 1 per 500, or reply rate below 1% after 1,500 sends Cut the tier and rework the trigger.
Tiers 1 and 3 below 1 per 250 Held to a higher bar. A dated obligation and a published last-time-buy date should beat this - so the fault is the copy or the persona resolution, not the signal.
