01

The routing signal

Every account is routed on the platform bet it has already publicly placed. Bought a portal, hiring to build one, already built one, about to need one, or hasn't started.

strongest signal
01

Job title fails. A Head of Platform running Backstage and one running raw Terraform need completely different emails. Same title, different conversation.

02

Industry fails. A fintech and a gaming company at the same platform maturity have the identical problem. The vertical adds nothing.

03

Headcount fails. It predicts budget, not urgency. A 900-engineer company with a stable platform is a worse lead than a 200-engineer company that just opened a platform req.

04

Platform maturity works. It determines the pain, the objection and the value prop simultaneously, and the prospect publishes it themselves in GitHub, in job reqs, and in conference talks.

02

The lead-listing engine

GitHub code search plus job-requisition body text delivers the market and the routing signal in one pull. No intent vendor, no enrichment credits.

first match wins 
1. IF idp_tool_detected is not 'none' (Backstage, Port, Cortex, OpsLevel, Humanitec, Spacelift, env0) -> Tier 1 - Portal already in place (A detected competitor tool is the strongest observable commitment. Money is already moving, the category is already understood, and the conversation starts at 'which layer' rather than 'why platform engineering'.)
2. IF NOT Tier 1 AND platform_req_open is TRUE -> Tier 2 - Hiring to build the platform (The requisition is the build decision, caught before the cost is sunk. There is a live window before the req is filled and the 18 months start.)
3. IF NOT Tier 1-2 AND homegrown_iac_evidence is not 'none' -> Tier 3 - Homegrown platform at scale (Real commitment, but sunk. The conversation is displacement rather than avoidance, and the tone has to respect the build.)
4. IF NOT Tier 1-3 AND (cloud_count >= 2 OR Series B+ raise within 12 months) -> Tier 4 - Sprawl in motion (The pain is imminent rather than present. Environments are about to multiply faster than the infra team can scale.)
5. IF NOT Tier 1-4 AND eng_headcount >= 100 -> Tier 5 - Catch-all, toil ratio (Genuine fit with no timing signal. The catch-all guarantees 100% coverage so no account in the TAM is left unrouted.)
6. IF eng_headcount < 100 and no signal above -> Suppress (Below the fit floor. Not enough environment complexity for the platform argument to land.)

Launch plan

Tier 2 goes first, not Tier 1. The highest-intent list is the smallest, and burning it on an unvalidated framework is the expensive mistake.

launch first
01

Weeks 1-3, Tier 2 only at 150 sends/day. Highest urgency and the largest launchable tier. This validates the copy framework before spending Tier 1's scarce list on it. Normally the highest-intent tier goes first, but Tier 1 is only ~4,750 contacts and burning it on an untested framework is the expensive mistake.

02

Weeks 4-6, add Tier 1 at 300 sends/day. The smallest and most valuable list, launched second on a framework already proven. Run both Tier 1 variants as a chaos test: adopters-file evidence against repository evidence.

03

Weeks 7-10, add Tier 3 at 500 sends/day. Broadest high-quality signal tier, and the first one where volume starts to carry the number.

04

Weeks 11-14, add Tier 4 at 700 sends/day. Volume tier. Expect a lower reply ratio than Tiers 1-3 by design.

05

Week 15 onward, add Tier 5 at 1,000+ sends/day. Full cascade, all 95,000 contacts routed, 100% TAM coverage.

06

Week 20 onward, layer in the side campaigns (new Head of Platform, conference follow-up, analyst nurture). Only once the waterfall itself is stable.

07

Prerequisites before week 1: an approved price anchor for the build-vs-buy model, a published portal-coexistence architecture page for Tier 1 to link to, the cost model asset built, the weekly job-req re-scrape running, and open tracking disabled on all sending infrastructure.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacts Perfect. Scale this tier hard.
1 per 300 Good. Let it run untouched.
1 per 450 to 500 Borderline. Test the second copy variant before touching anything else.
Worse than 1 per 500, or reply rate under 1% after 1,500+ sends Cut the tier and rework. Never judge a tier before 1,500 sends.
Lead-magnet request rate under 60% of positive replies The artifact is the CTA, so this is the real leading indicator. Rework the magnet, not the email.
Spam-complaint rate above 0.1% Pause immediately. This audience reports rather than ignores, and it damages sending infrastructure fast.
