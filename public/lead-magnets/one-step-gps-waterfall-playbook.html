Signal

The signal is what the fleet's public FMCSA record already says

The FMCSA Company Census plus the SMS violation and crash files return the TAM and the routing signal in one free pull: power units, drivers, address, phone, HOS violations, Unsafe Driving percentile, crashes, and unit growth. No paid enrichment needed to route a lead.

strongest signal
01

The FMCSA Company Census File on data.transportation.gov is the TAM and the routing signal in one free pull. FMCSA A&I Registration Statistics puts active motor carrier companies with USDOT numbers at roughly 2.1 million (snapshots in 2026 range from about 2.08M to 2.23M depending on file date), with power units, driver count, legal name, DBA, address, and phone already on the record. No paid enrichment is needed before a lead is routed.

02

The SMS violation, inspection, and crash files attach to the same USDOT number, so compliance heat and safety exposure are observable presence signals, not inferred absence. A recorded hours-of-service violation proves the logging problem existed at a roadside stop. That is exactly the reliability bar the waterfall method requires, unlike guessing which telematics vendor a fleet uses today.

03

The ladder maps one-to-one onto One Step GPS's published offers: FMCSA-registered ELD/HOS at $20.95 with GPS for the HOS tier, AI dashcams at $29.95 with in-cab coaching and 28+ insurance integrations for the safety tier, $13.95 flat with add-and-drop devices and no penalty for the growth tier, and the published-pricing story for the catch-all. Every tier's value prop is lifted from onestepgps.com/about/company-facts-data.

01

FMCSA MCMIS Company Census File (data.transportation.gov)

The TAM spine plus in_tam, usdot_number, power_units, driver_count, legal name, DBA, physical address, phone, and MCS-150 filing date. Free bulk download. About 2.1 million active USDOT registrations, of which roughly 701k are for-hire with interstate authority and about 1.48M are private carriers.

02

FMCSA SMS BASIC percentiles + inspection and violation files

hos_violation_12mo, hos_basic_percentile, unsafe_driving_basic_percentile, and the specific violation codes (395.x hours-of-service and ELD, speeding, reckless, distracted). This is the Tier 1 and Tier 2 routing signal, keyed on the same USDOT number.

03

FMCSA crash file / SAFER Company Snapshot

crash_count_24mo and the human-readable snapshot used for QA on any account before a send. Confirms the record state a first line will quote.

04

MCS-150 revision history (census deltas over time)

power_unit_growth_pct and new_authority_date for Tier 3. Comparing two census snapshots is free and shows which fleets actually added units rather than which ones say they are growing.

05

Google Maps / Places by trade category and metro

The non-DOT half of the catch-all: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, pest control, towing, mobile services, and equipment rental fleets that operate vehicles but never file with FMCSA. Returns name, phone, website, and location count in the same pull.

06

Apollo / LinkedIn Sales Nav (after routing)

buyer_title and work email for an already-routed account: Owner, Fleet Manager, Director of Operations, Safety Director, VP Operations, Fleet Supervisor. Contact resolution last, never the TAM spine on its own.

07

Indeed / LinkedIn job posts (optional Tier 3 confirmation)

hiring_drivers as a secondary growth confirmation, and any incumbent telematics tool named in a dispatcher or fleet-manager job description. Presence only. Never route on the absence of a named tool.

08

One Step GPS customer suppression list

already_customer exclusion, supplied by One Step GPS before launch. With 20,000+ active fleets on the platform this matters more than usual. Suppress, do not tier.

First match wins. Fleets with fresh hours-of-service violations go first, then fleets with safety or crash exposure, then fleets that just grew or just registered, then every remaining commercial fleet including the non-DOT trades fleets that never appear on the census.
