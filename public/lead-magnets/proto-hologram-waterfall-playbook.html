Prepared for Proto Hologram

The Proto Hologram outbound journey.

One priority-routed cascade across every physical space in North America, built on a signal nobody sells.

Core insight: Every competitor in this category sells a box, which means every competitor has to convince a buyer to find new capital for a new object. Proto is the only vendor with an appliance that upgrades the screens a prospect already paid for. That single fact turns the cold opener from "buy a $50,000 hologram" into "make the screens you already own talk back" - a capex fight becomes an upgrade to a budget line that already exists and already has an owner. So ProtoPlayer leads and Luma follows.

Signal

One question routes the entire market

Segmenting Proto by vertical looks obvious and is the trap. Verticals are orthogonal, an account can qualify for four at once, and you end up maintaining eight campaigns that decay together. One commitment ladder replaces all of them.

strongest signal
01

It ladders. "Already bought a hologram" down to "owns a building with footfall and hasn't thought about this yet" is a genuine priority order, not a set of parallel buckets.

02

It cuts across verticals instead of along them. A 40-screen network in a hospital lobby and a 40-screen network in a mall have the same pain and the same answer, so vertical becomes a merge field rather than a campaign.

03

Every value proposition Proto already ships maps to exactly one rung. ProtoPlayer to installed screens, always-on avatars to unstaffed counters, Luma rental to a date on the calendar.

04

It is answerable for free, for every account, from public pages - so the unit economics of list building never break.
