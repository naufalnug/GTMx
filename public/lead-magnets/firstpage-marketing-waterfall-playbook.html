01

Google Ads Transparency Center

Free, no login. Proves a business is running ads right now and shows the creative. The only public record that confirms a marketing budget already exists. Caveat: only verified advertisers appear — treat a hit as strong signal, a miss as no signal.

02

Chrome UX Report (CrUX) API

Free with an API key, updated daily, ~15M origins. Real field Core Web Vitals split by mobile and desktop. Missing from CrUX means under ~100 visits/month — the absence is the loudest signal available.

03

PageSpeed Insights API

The fallback for low-traffic domains CrUX will not report. Runs a fresh test on any URL regardless of traffic, so no account is ever left unscored.

04

Google Business Profile / Maps

Local pack position, review count and recency, response rate, categories, service list, photo recency. For trades, health, legal and auto in the Fraser Valley, the map pack is the funnel — and every input Google ranks on is public.

05

The prospect's own website

Mandatory under CASL anyway. Yields the published address that forms the consent basis, plus SSL, mobile responsiveness, platform age, copyright year, tappable phone, and whether the form actually submits.

06

Statistics Canada — Canadian Business Counts

Free TAM sizing by industry code, employment band and municipality, twice a year. Vancouver CMA: 120,047 businesses with employees. Abbotsford-Mission: 8,370. A counting source, not a contact source.

02

How the market routes

Priority-ordered, first match wins, one tier per account, re-scored quarterly. Five tiers cover 100% of the qualified market.

first match wins 
1. IF Live Google advertiser AND (mobile LCP > 4.0s OR PageSpeed mobile < 40 OR no tappable phone above fold OR broken form) -> Tier 1 — Paid leak (Budget is confirmed and the waste is arithmetic. They are buying the click twice: once from Google, again in the bounce.)
2. IF Local pack position 4+ for money term AND 2+ named competitors within 5km in the top 3 AND at least one measurable profile gap -> Tier 2 — Map-pack gap (The map pack is the whole funnel for local trades and services, and the comparison is emotionally sharp — you can name the actual competitor taking the calls.)
3. IF No SSL OR not mobile-responsive OR discontinued platform OR copyright year 3+ years stale OR absent from CrUX OR PageSpeed mobile < 40 -> Tier 3 — Site liability (Undeniable, screenshot-able evidence. Sells the higher-ticket build rather than the retainer.)
4. IF Hiring a marketing/sales/estimator role OR new location OR new business licence OR rebrand OR award or major contract in last 90 days -> Tier 4 — Change event (Timing beats targeting. A business opening a second location has a marketing problem it cannot postpone.)
5. IF Passes the qualification gate but no tier above fired -> Tier 5 — Catch-all (100% coverage. No qualified account is silently dropped, and the honesty angle is the most differentiated sentence available.)

Launch plan

Six weeks. Past-client reactivation runs in week one because it is legal today and needs no infrastructure at all.

launch first
01

Week 0 - Close the three launch blockers: two named-client outcome metrics, the average client tenure figure, and the CASL consent register. Launch past-client reactivation immediately, because CASL grants implied consent for 24 months after an existing business relationship and it needs no new infrastructure at all.

02

Week 1 - Acquire and configure two to three secondary sending domains, never firstpagemarketing.com. Begin mailbox warmup. Stand up the consent register with screenshot, source URL and harvest date per address.

03

Week 2 - Build the list script. Run the Fraser Valley and Lower Mainland grid across Maps, CrUX, PageSpeed and the ad library. Score every account and route it to exactly one tier.

04

Week 3 - Produce the first 100 Tier 2 Local Pack Reports. Write and internally test the Tier 2 sequence against the copy contract.

05

Week 4 - Launch Tier 2 at 500 contacts. Hand-work the first 40 Tier 1 accounts in parallel.

06

Week 5 - Read the reply data and rewrite whatever missed. Scale Tier 2 to 1,500. Launch Tier 1.

07

Week 6 - Launch Tier 3 and Tier 4. Hold the total at 2,500-3,000 new contacts per month; the list is finite and burns out in ten weeks at spray cadence.

08

Ongoing - Run the LinkedIn lane against the roughly 7,000 fit accounts that publish a form instead of an address and therefore fail the CASL gate. Same routing, same findings, no consent constraint on a connection request.

Scale / cut rules

Delivery below 97% Stop sending on that domain. Audit SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment before another send goes out - an agency selling marketing that lands in spam is disproving its own pitch.
Tier 2 reply below 5% at 60 days Pause and rewrite. Tier 2 is the volume engine; if the named-competitor finding is not landing, nothing downstream will.
Tier 2 reply at or above 8% Scale to the full 3,600-account tier and bring Tier 4 forward a week.
Tier 1 reply below 6% Check the ad-library matching first. A miss usually means the advertiser is unverified rather than not advertising, and a wrong claim in this tier is expensive.
Tier 3 reply below 2.5% Switch the primary angle from speed to the hiring consequence. Owners who have accepted a bad site have not accepted losing tradespeople.
Positive share of replies below 20% The finding is landing but the offer is not. Tighten the artefact, never lengthen the email.
Spam complaints above 0.08% Halt the tier immediately. Re-check that every address in the batch has a logged consent record with source URL and harvest date.
Asset requests flat for two weeks This is the leading indicator and it moves two weeks ahead of booked consultations. Treat a flat line as the earliest honest signal that the finding has stopped being specific enough.
