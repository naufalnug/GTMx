02 / Routing

First match wins, and nothing falls out the bottom

Every account is tested top-to-bottom and routes to exactly one tier. The catch-all condition is simply membership in the market, so coverage is 100% by construction.

first match wins 
1. IF A voice-AI vendor is detected (Bland, Vapi, Retell, Replicant, LiveKit, Twilio Programmable Voice) -> Tier 1 — DIY Voice Stack (They already bought the model. What they did not buy is the phone system underneath it, which is precisely what Bigly sells.)
2. IF No voice-AI vendor, but a dialer or contact-centre platform is detected -> Tier 2 — Headcount-Capped Dialing (Dial capacity is a function of headcount, and the numbers are burning faster than they can be replaced.)
3. IF Neither of the above, but TrustedForm or Jornaya is present on the lead form -> Tier 3 — Paid-Lead Buyer (They pay per lead, so every minute between form-fill and first ring destroys economics they have already paid for.)
4. IF None of the above, but there are open phone-role requisitions in the last 60 days -> Tier 4 — Hiring the Problem (They are solving a call-volume problem with headcount — three to six weeks to ramp, and coverage that stops with the shift.)
5. IF Everything else in the twelve verticals with a public phone number and a web lead form -> Tier 5 — Catch-all: Phone-Dependent (The business runs on the phone but only staffs it forty hours a week. After-hours enquiries die in voicemail.)

06 / Launch

Prove it on the sharpest tier first

Tier 1 validates the positioning fastest. Tier 3 carries the strongest economic argument. The catch-all becomes the always-on volume floor once the signal tiers have produced winning copy.

launch first
01

Weeks 1-2 — Launch Tier 1. Smallest market, sharpest pain, and the fastest read on whether the "we are the layer your stack is missing" wedge lands. It also validates Bigly's core positioning most directly.

02

Weeks 2-4 — Add Tier 3 in parallel once Tier 1 copy is stable. The page-source scrape is cheap and the economic argument is the most quantifiable of the five.

03

Weeks 4-6 — Bring on Tier 2, then Tier 4. Refresh the Tier 4 requisition scrape weekly; reqs go stale inside 60 days.

04

Week 6 onward — Open Tier 5 as the always-on volume floor, borrowing the winning copy patterns from the four signal tiers.

05

Ongoing — Refresh Tiers 1 to 3 monthly. Judge each tier against its own threshold, never blended. Two copy variants per tier maximum.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacts Perfect. Scale the tier hard and expand the source.
1 per 300 Good. Let it run untouched.
1 per 450-500 Borderline. Test one copy variant — vary the magnet or the notice line, hold everything else constant.
Worse than 1 per 500, or reply rate under 1% after 1,500+ sends Failing. Cut the tier and rework the trigger.
