Waterfall

One cascade routes every licensed operator in GrowFlow states

First match wins. Dutchie-family POS first, then other retail POS, then adjacent marketplace/menu-only stacks, then every remaining licensed operator in GrowFlow's footprint.

first match wins 
1. IF in_tam AND competitor_pos_family = dutchie (Dutchie, Greenbits, LeafLogix / Dutchie POS) AND not already_customer -> Tier 1 - Dutchie Switch + Migration Brief (They already pay for the market-leading retail ecosystem. Strongest switch intent for GrowFlow Retail + compliance (Metrc/BioTrack/CCRS) with a migration magnet.)
2. IF in_tam AND competitor_pos_family = other_retail_pos (Flowhub, Treez, Meadow, Blaze, Cova, BioTrackTHC retail, MJ Freeway/Akerna legacy) AND not already_customer -> Tier 2 - Compliance vs Ops Comparison (They run a competing retail POS. Side-by-side GrowFlow pitch on effortless compliance, inventory, and hours saved — matching Allison's compliance vs efficiency A/B.)
3. IF in_tam AND competitor_pos_family = adjacent_stack (Leaflink-only, Nabis-only, Weedmaps/Leafly menu-only, SpringBig-only, Bleaum-only, no full POS brand) AND not already_customer -> Tier 3 - Marketplace + Wholesale Brief (They have ordering/menu/loyalty tools without a unified seed-to-sale POS. Maps to GrowFlow Live Marketplace + Wholesale/Retail consolidation.)
4. IF in_tam AND none of the above -> Catch-all - GrowFlow Compliance Playbook (Still in TAM (licensed operators in GrowFlow states). Softest CTA around compliance automation, hours back, and seed-to-consumer clarity. Mandatory 100% coverage.)
