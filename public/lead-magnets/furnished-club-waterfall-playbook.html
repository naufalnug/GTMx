Prepared for Furnished Club

The Furnished Club waterfall journey.

A supply-first outbound system for a marketplace with 14 cities and 56 rooms.

Core insight: Furnished Club is supply-constrained, not demand-constrained. 01 - Signal

The routing signal is published by the prospect, for free

Every furnished property publishes its own qualification: channel presence, booking engine, minimum stay, monthly discount, calendar. A boutique hotel on Booking.com and nowhere else has already described the problem Furnished Club solves. No vendor sells this list, and it refreshes daily.

strongest signal
01

It is free. Channel presence, booking-engine detection, minimum stay and monthly-discount settings are all readable from public listing pages.

02

It is unbuyable. No enrichment vendor sells 'appears on Booking but not Expedia, and takes reservations by email'.

03

It is dated. NYC's Local Law 18 and Mexico City's 180-night cap put an external clock on the two biggest tiers.

04

It generates the offer. The same sweep that qualifies the lead produces the Gap Report we send them.

02 - Waterfall

One cascade, first match wins, no lead counted twice

Routing precedence runs by value per win. Launch order runs by speed to build. Those are two different sequences, and conflating them is the most common way a waterfall fails.

first match wins 
1. IF Independent boutique hotel or aparthotel, 8-40 rooms, present on one or two OTA channels only, with no booking engine detected on the direct site -> Tier 1 - Distribution Gap (Highest value per win. One signature brings 10-30 rooms, and they already pay 15-18% to Booking for a fraction of the demand.)
2. IF Operator or small PMC with 5-50 furnished units, portfolio size visible on a host profile or their own site -> Tier 2 - Multi-Unit Operator (They have the units and the operations. What they lack is the corporate back office - invoicing, POs, 30-day terms. 10+ units routes to White-Label.)
3. IF Furnished unit in NYC or Mexico City with regulation-driven evidence of displacement (minimum stay reset to 30+, absent from the registry, or long blocked calendar stretches) -> Tier 3 - Regulation Displaced (The urgency is external and time-boxed. They have a furnished asset and a legally mandated hole in the calendar.)
4. IF Property announced, under construction, or accepting reservations for a future opening date -> Tier 4 - Pre-Opening (Zero switching cost and the highest close rate in the cascade - but only if we arrive before they hire someone.)
5. IF Furnished single unit with a 1-2 night minimum, no monthly discount, and an open calendar 30-60 days out -> Tier 5 - Underperforming Unit (Three public fields say this owner does not know the 30+ day market exists. Volume ballast, not a priority tier.)
6. IF Any furnished-housing operator in the 14 marketed cities that matched none of the above -> Catch-All - Independent Operators (Nothing addressable gets dropped, and there is always volume to keep the infrastructure warm and the learning loop running.)

Supply first. Demand geo-gated behind an inventory threshold

A market does not open for corporate housing outbound until it holds 25+ bookable units with confirmed 30+ day pricing. Today that is Mexico City and nothing else. Selling housing you cannot deliver burns the exact buyers who buy on reliability.

05

06

07

Day 90 review - three questions. Rooms signed by tier, so is the routing precedence right? Which single objection kills the most deals, and is it a copy problem or a product problem? Which market is closest to clearing the demand gate, and what does it need?

Scale / cut rules

Delivery below 97% Stop sending. This is infrastructure, not copy. Nothing downstream is measurable until it clears 97%.
Bounce above 3% Pause the campaign and re-verify the list. Independent hotel contact data degrades fast and unverified sends cost domain reputation.
Tier 1 reply below 4% The channel-gap claim is wrong or stale. Re-run the sweep before touching the copy - a false 'you're not on Expedia' kills the campaign instantly.
Tier 1 reply above 8% Scale the list build to the remaining marketed cities and add the LinkedIn layer at full coverage.
Tier 4 reply below 8% The send is arriving too late. Move the trigger earlier relative to the opening date - the entire value of this tier is the window.
Positive reply share below 25% Wrong tier or wrong claim, not wrong subject line. Re-check the routing before rewriting anything.
Catch-all reply within 1 point of a signal tier That tier's signal is doing no work. Rebuild the signal or fold the tier into the catch-all.
Unsubscribe above 1% Targeting problem, not a copy problem. Tighten the tier filter.
Any market reaches 25+ bookable units with confirmed 30+ day pricing Unlock demand tiers D1 and D4 for that market only. Until then, demand outbound stays closed.
Signed units below 8 per month at day 60 Shift budget from Tier 3 volume to Tier 1 and Tier 2 depth. Rooms per contract matters more than replies per send.
