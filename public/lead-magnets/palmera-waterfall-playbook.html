The signal

One signal routes the entire market

Commitment to a Spanish-speaking market leaves a public paper trail before the marketing ever starts. Trademark filings, ad transparency registries, careers pages, product localisation and retail distribution are all observable for free, all dated, and none of them are sold as a list by any data vendor.

strongest signal
01

The Meta Ad Library is free, public, needs no login, and filters by language. One lookup proves a live paid budget AND proves the Spanish-language gap in the same pull.

02

It is not a proxy. It is the brand's own live creative, which means the email can reference an ad the recipient personally approved.

03

Nobody sells 'brands spending on paid social with under 5% Spanish creative', because nobody has packaged it. The list cannot be bought, so it cannot be copied.

04

The same sweep is the lead magnet. Research artefact and offer are the same object, so the offer is free to produce at any volume.

The routing

Gate first, route second

Every account is tested top to bottom and lands in exactly one tier. A legal filing outranks live ad spend, which outranks a funded headcount, which outranks a localised product, which outranks shelf space, which outranks nothing at all.

first match wins 
1. IF GATE: budget proof AND market relevance AND target vertical AND NOT an agency or peer AND NOT a named multicultural AOR -> Enter the waterfall, otherwise discard or route to Side Campaign S1 (PALM ERA services brands like AT&T, Acer and TCL. Routing accounts that cannot fund a real retainer burns sends and depresses every tier's measured performance. All marketing, advertising, media, PR and production agencies are excluded before any tier test - they are peers, not prospects.)
2. IF LATAM trademark filed in the last 9 months, OR ccTLD registered in the last 12 months, OR Google ATC shows LATAM region buying -> Tier 1 - Filed Intent (A legal filing means money has been spent, counsel has been engaged, and a commercial launch is 6 to 12 months out. At that exact moment there is no in-market creative, no media plan and no local partner. Highest intent in the entire market.)
3. This is a yield problem on committed budget, not a new line item - and nobody can report on the Spanish-language segment because it does not exist to measure.)
4. IF NOT Tiers 1-2, AND a multicultural, Hispanic or LATAM requisition is open within 120 days, OR that title is already filled -> Tier 3 - Mandate Without Machinery (A requisition is an approved budget line, so the mandate is funded. But one person cannot produce research, creative, social, media and post-production. Either the req has sat open in a thin talent market, or the new hire has no bench.)
5. 7. IF Everything else that clears the gate -> Tier 6 - Catch-All, Category Benchmark (No observable commitment yet, so the pain is inferred rather than evidenced. Start with a number, not a pitch. This is the tier that guarantees 100% TAM coverage.)

The campaigns

What actually gets sent

The research artefact and the offer are the same object. The Ad Library sweep that builds the list also produces the lead magnet, which means the offer costs nothing incremental to produce at scale.

Niche Niche

The Filing Clock

The highest-intent moment in the entire category. Legal spend is committed, a launch sits two to three quarters out, and there is no in-market agency yet. Hand-worked at low volume because filings arrive continuously - a permanent trickle rather than a batch.

Filter
US or global brand with a trademark application filed at IMPI, INPI or SIC in the last 9 months, cross-checked for advertising activity.
AI strategy
Pull the filed mark name, the registry, the filing month and the Nice class. Reference the specific mark in the opening line. The filing itself is the personalisation - no enrichment required, and no competitor is watching this registry for marketing intent.
Value
Make money
Niche Niche

Domain Registered, Nothing Live

A cheaper, faster variant of the filing signal. Works especially well for digital-first brands that will not file a trademark until much later in the process.

Filter
New .mx, .com.br or .com.co registration in the last 12 months where the domain resolves to a holding page or a redirect.
AI strategy
WHOIS date plus a live check on what the domain actually serves. A registered country domain with nothing behind it is a launch in progress with a shorter fuse than a trademark filing.
Value
Save time

The Category Benchmark

Largest tier and the one most sensitive to the proof gaps, so it launches last. No pitch and no call - just where they rank against five named peers. Most people who ask for it are surprised in one direction or the other.

Filter
Everything that clears the qualification gate with no observable commitment signal.
AI strategy
Group accounts by category and build a five-peer in-language presence ranking from the same Ad Library and hreflang sweep. The email offers a number rather than a service, and the number is generated, not written.
Value
Save time
Niche Niche

Heritage Month Reverse Timing (side campaign)

A side campaign, not part of the waterfall. The single highest-leverage seasonal window in the category, and it is almost entirely uncontested because every competitor pitches it too late.

Filter
Gated accounts in consumer categories, prospected in April and May rather than August.
AI strategy
Hispanic Heritage Month runs 15 September to 15 October. Brands scrambling in August have already lost the year. Brands briefed in spring have budget and runway - so the outreach window is five months ahead of the moment everyone else sells into.
Value
Save time

The launch

Nine weeks, phased

Tier 2 launches first, not Tier 1. Its TAM is five times larger so it produces readable signal fastest, its lead magnet is auto-generated from the same sweep that built the list, and it proves the central thesis every other tier borrows.

launch first
01

Weeks 1-2, build. Close the proof gaps first - no published metric, no Hispanic lift number and no LATAM entry outcome are all hard blockers. Stand up 15 domains and 45 mailboxes, run the Ad Library and hreflang sweep on the first 8,000 accounts, and build the Tier 2 gap-report template so it is generated rather than written.

02

Week 3, launch Tier 2 first - not Tier 1. Its TAM is five times larger so it produces readable signal fastest, its lead magnet is auto-generated from the same sweep that built the list, and it proves the central thesis every other tier borrows. Start at 1,500 contacts and hold until the reply ratio is readable.

03

Week 3 in parallel, run Tier 1 by hand. Small, scarce, highest intent. Filings arrive continuously, so this becomes a permanent trickle rather than a batch.

04

Weeks 5-6, add Tier 3 then Tier 4, once Tier 2 has a winning copy variant. Tier 3's two openers - requisition open versus title filled - are meaningfully different emails and must be measured separately.

05

Weeks 7-8, add Tier 5 then Tier 6. Tier 6 is last and largest, and it is the tier most sensitive to the proof gaps, so it should not launch until those are closed.

06

Week 9 onward, start the side campaigns. Existing-client expansion into sister business units at AT&T, Acer, TCL, Fortinet and Bimbo comes first - it is warm and costs almost nothing. Then Heritage Month reverse timing, prospected in April and May rather than August.

07

Volume math for 12 qualified meetings a month: at 1 positive reply per 250 contacts and 45% of positive replies converting to a held meeting, that is roughly 6,750 new contacts per month, 20,250 sends, about 920 per business day, 37 mailboxes minimum. Build 45 mailboxes across 15 domains for headroom and warm them for three weeks before week 3.

08

Route Spanish and Portuguese sends through dedicated mailboxes so language-specific deliverability stays isolated from the English pool. Write each email in the recipient's working language, never the campaign's subject language - the buyer of Hispanic marketing is very often a general-market CMO who does not speak Spanish.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacts Perfect. Scale the tier hard and add sending capacity.
1 per 300 Good. Let it run untouched.
1 per 450 to 500 Borderline. Test one copy variant and hold everything else constant.
Worse than 1 per 500, or reply rate under 1% after 1,500 sends Failing. Cut the tier and rework the trigger, not the copy.
