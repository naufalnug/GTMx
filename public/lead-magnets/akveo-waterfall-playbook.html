Waterfall

One cascade routes every in-TAM mid-market buyer

First match wins. Open AI/ML roles first, then software-engineering IC roles, then product/engineering leadership hiring, then every remaining in-TAM company across Akveo’s named verticals.

first match wins 
1. IF in_tam AND ai_role_open = true (LinkedIn Jobs / careers lists AI Engineer, ML Engineer, LLM / GenAI, Data Scientist / Data Engineer for AI, RAG, Prompt Engineer, or equivalent) AND not already_customer -> Tier 1 - AI PoC / Agentic Brief (They are already buying AI build capacity via hiring. Strongest intent for Akveo’s AI PoC (typically $15K, 4–6 weeks), Agentic AI / RAG, or AI-Augmented Engineers (1–2 week onboard) versus a 3–6 month hire cycle. Proof: Factory Copilot 50% faster data retrieval; Wealthfluent 100% regulatory compliance.)
2. IF in_tam AND eng_ic_role_open = true (software IC: Full-Stack, Backend, Frontend, Mobile, Platform, DevOps) AND no Tier-1 AI roles AND not already_customer -> Tier 2 - Dedicated Team Brief (They are hiring builders, not AI specialists — pain is backlog and time-to-ship. Maps to Dedicated Team in 7 days and Production Build from $60k / 3–5 months on akveo.com. 2x faster delivery claim and 200+ projects are the proof path.)
3. IF in_tam AND leadership_role_open = true (CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Product / Digital / Innovation, Engineering Manager) AND no Tier 1–2 IC / AI roles AND not already_customer -> Tier 3 - AI Opportunity Audit (They are buying leadership capacity or replacing a tech lead — softest high-intent before catch-all. Maps to AI Consulting (2 weeks, fixed fee) and a scoped PoC recommendation, not a full production pitch on Day 0.)
4. IF in_tam AND none of the above -> Catch-all - AI-Native Build Playbook (Still in TAM (named verticals, mid-market digital buyers). Soft CTA around AI-native builds, modernization, and a free first conversation. Mandatory 100% coverage.)
