Prepared for Cardwell Beach

The Cardwell Beach waterfall journey.

One cascade across six verticals. 36,000 qualified accounts. A routing signal that costs nothing to read and cannot be bought.

Core insight: Cardwell Beach does not sell a campaign, it sells a replacement for a function. So the right routing signal is not what a company does, it is what its marketing is visibly failing to do. Capacity failure is public: an org that stopped advertising, or is running the same two ads it built nine months ago, or has had a marketing director role open since spring, is publishing its own diagnosis. Verticals are the list-pull axis. Execution state is the routing axis.

The signal

A job posting is a free discovery call transcript.

Twelve states now mandate the salary band inside the posting itself. That turns a public job ad into a priced, dated, fully-specified brief: the disciplines they need, the tools they run, the KPIs, and the money already approved. Stack the ad transparency libraries on top and every organization's marketing execution state becomes readable for free.

strongest signal
01

It is free, dated and timestamped. Google Ads Transparency Center and Meta Ad Library are public archives of every verified advertiser, filterable by custom date range and refreshed within 24 to 48 hours.

02

It cannot be bought. No enrichment vendor sells the fact that a company stopped advertising 74 days ago, or that its only two live creatives have not changed since March.

03

It cuts across verticals instead of along them. A hospice and a botanical garden in the same execution state are far more alike as prospects than two botanical gardens in different states.

04

It refreshes itself. Requisitions post and close continuously, ad libraries update every other day, seasons recur annually. An account re-routes from Tier 3 to Tier 2 the moment it goes dark, with no human decision.

01

Google Ads Transparency Center + Meta Ad Library

The routing signal for three of five tiers. Public archives of every verified advertiser showing when they started, what they ran, and when they stopped. Free, filterable by custom date range, refreshed within 24 to 48 hours.

02

Public ATS boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) + Indeed + LinkedIn Jobs

Open marketing requisitions with days-open, discipline count, and the mandated salary band in twelve states (CA, CO, CT, HI, IL, ME, MD, MA, MN, NJ, NY, WA). The rule follows remote roles into those states.

03

SAMHSA N-SUMHSS National Directory (findtreatment.gov)

Every licensed substance use and mental health facility in the US, with services, setting and payment types. Free Excel and PDF. Updated weekly for changes, monthly for new facilities.

04

ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer API

Every 501(c)(3) and 501(c)(6) that e-filed a 990: total revenue, dues revenue, advertising and promotion expense, plus officer names and compensation. Free, no API key required.

05

IMLS Museum Universe Data File + AZA + AAM accreditation lists

Every US museum with revenue banding. AZA publishes 240 accredited institutions plus 14 related facilities; AAM roughly 1,100. Accreditation doubles as a budget floor filter.

06

CMS Provider of Services + Hospice and Home Health Care Compare

Every certified hospice, home health agency and skilled nursing facility, with ownership, chain affiliation and star ratings.

07

AAFD chapter directory + FDD filings

Independent franchisee associations and multi-unit operators. Direct structural lookalikes of the McDonald's National Owners Association, the strongest single credential in the portfolio.

08

The organization's own calendar, press and permit filings

Dated public commitments: season openers, exhibitions, new locations, annual meetings, facility launches. A published date is a clock nobody had to pay for.

The routing

One cascade, not six vertical campaigns.

Six verticals means six orthogonal campaigns that all decay at once. Routing on execution state instead cuts across verticals, so a museum, a treatment center and a franchisee co-op that all went dark on paid last quarter receive the same argument with different evidence.

first match wins 
1. IF NOT budget_qualified (revenue under $2M, or fewer than 3 locations) -> EXCLUDE (Below the under-$10K/month engagement floor. Gate applied before any tier routing so the cascade never spends volume on accounts that cannot buy.)
2. IF open_marketing_req = true AND (req_days_open >= 45 OR reposted) AND req_discipline_count >= 4 -> Tier 1 - The Unfillable Req (They committed budget to one generalist who would have to be six specialists. The market has already told them that person does not exist at that price.)
3. IF ELSE IF google_ads_status = stopped OR meta_ads_status = stopped, with last_seen between 30 and 270 days ago -> Tier 2 - Went Dark (Nobody chose to stop. The person running it left, budget was redirected mid-quarter, or an agency was cut. Demand did not pause with the media.)
4. IF ELSE IF ads live AND live_creative_count <= 2 AND oldest_live_creative_first_seen >= 90 days ago -> Tier 3 - Running On Fumes (Not a strategy decision, a capacity ceiling. A one or two person team can keep a campaign switched on but cannot build the next set, add a channel, or produce video.)
5. IF ELSE IF dated_commitment IS NOT NULL AND commitment_weeks_out between 4 and 16 AND no ad activity references it -> Tier 4 - The Clock With No Campaign (The date is fixed and the campaign is not built. Briefing and onboarding an agency takes longer than the window they have left.)
6. IF ELSE (budget-qualified, no detectable paid activity anywhere) -> Tier 5 - The Quiet Operator (catch-all) (Either it genuinely works without paid, or paid keeps sliding to next quarter because nobody owns it. Usually the second. Guarantees 100% TAM coverage.)

The tiers

Five tiers, exclusive by construction.

Tier 2 requires a stopped advertiser and Tier 3 requires a live one, so the two can never both match. Every budget-qualified account exits the matrix with exactly one tier assignment, and the catch-all guarantees nothing is stranded.

The launch

Launch order is deliberately not priority order.

Tier 1 has the sharpest economics but the thinnest proof. Tier 4 has hard named numbers. The first six weeks buy credibility for everything after them, so cultural destinations go first.

launch first
01

Weeks 1 to 6 - Tier 4, cultural destinations only (~2,900 contacts). Launch here rather than Tier 1, because this is the only tier where the proof is hard, named and numeric: The Mob Museum at more than 20,000 visitors and a Gold ADDY, Idaho Botanical Garden at 19 percent. Fastest credible win, and it produces the first reference story for everything downstream.

02

Weeks 5 to 12 - Tier 2, all verticals. The largest high-intent pool, and every account in it has already proven it will spend. The Go-Dark Report is the most persuasive free artifact in the build. Launch the behavioral health slice as its own variant with the compliance line.

03

Weeks 11 to 18 - Tier 1. Gated on proof gap 1 being closed. The economics are the sharpest argument available, but launching without one named client who replaced a hire means launching the core claim naked.

04

Weeks 17 to 24 - Tier 3. The broadest intent tier. Needs the creative-fatigue artifact built and templated before it goes live.

05

Week 23 onward - Tier 5, the catch-all. Largest volume, weakest signal, run last on the copy learnings from tiers one through four.

06

Week 9 onward - Side campaigns S1 to S4, only once the waterfall is stable: departure triggers, open marketing RFPs on procurement portals, franchisee association lookalikes hand-written president to president, and conference-triggered sends around ASAE, AAM, NatCon and Argentum.

07

Infrastructure from day one - 8 secondary domains, 3 mailboxes each, 25 sends per mailbox per day for 600 a day and roughly 12,000 a month. Three weeks of warmup before the first live send, warmup left on permanently. Full verification pass, 2 percent hard bounce ceiling. Scale domains before adding tiers, not after.

08

Guardrails applied throughout - No media performance number in any email until the client supplies a real one. No outcome or recovery-rate claims in behavioral health, and never imply lead purchasing. Open tracking off wherever is_gov_domain is true, since many municipal museums and zoos are public entities whose correspondence may be a public record.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacts Perfect. Scale that tier hard and add domains behind it.
1 per 300 Good. Let it run untouched.
1 per 450 to 500 Borderline. Test one copy variant, and swap the lead magnet before you touch the body.
Worse than 1 per 500, or reply rate under 1 percent after 1,500 sends Failing. Cut the tier and rework the trigger.
Reporting rule Report per tier, never in aggregate. A strong Tier 1 will otherwise hide a failing Tier 3 for months, and by the time the blended number moves, three months of volume has already gone to a trigger that does not work.
