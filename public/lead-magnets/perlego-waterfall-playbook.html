Prepared for Perlego

Your Perlego waterfall journey.

A scrollable GTMx strategy artifact for routing higher-education institutions, colleges, and academic departments into the right Perlego unlimited digital-library, instructor-pilot, or affordability offer. List building, campaign angles, and sample copy, prepared for Gauthier Van Malderen.

Core insight: Gauthier already sells an unlimited academic digital library so higher-ed students stop buying individual textbooks and institutions stop running course materials as per-title, concurrent-user, or credit-capped access. The one list is higher education only: universities, colleges, and academic departments that provision course materials or library access to learners — not multi-industry corporates, not a second publisher waterfall. The one routing signal is campus_course_materials_state: how the campus already puts textbooks in students' hands, because library/bookstore/LMS scrapes plus syllabus and affordability-page checks return a named inclusive-access or e-textbook competitor, a high-cost required reading list with no campus-wide unlimited library, a public textbook-affordability / WP / EDI page with no unlimited library named, or none of those, in one pull. Buyer title (librarian, faculty, student-success, procurement) is a merge tag after routing, not a second waterfall.

Signal

The signal is how the campus already puts textbooks in students' hands

A library, bookstore, or LMS scrape plus syllabus and affordability-page checks already name an inclusive-access or e-textbook competitor, a high-cost required reading list with no unlimited library, or a public equity/affordability page with no Perlego-class catalogue. That course-materials state is stronger than a title guess, and it is cheap to collect.

Waterfall

One cascade routes every in-TAM university, college, and department

First match wins. Named campus e-textbook / inclusive-access competitors go first, then departments with expensive required reading lists and no competitor platform, then institutions that publish textbook-affordability or WP pages without an unlimited library, then every remaining higher-ed account.

first match wins 
1. IF in_tam AND competitor_platform detected (VitalSource, Kortext, BibliU, RedShelf, Follett Inclusive Access, Pearson Inclusive Access, Cengage Unlimited campus program, Chegg eTextbooks, or another named inclusive-access / e-textbook platform on library, bookstore, or LMS pages) -> Tier 1 - Campus Switch Brief (They already pay to put digital textbooks on campus but typically still run per-title, concurrent-user, or credit-capped access. Strongest intent for Perlego's core institutional offer: unlimited access with no concurrency or credit limits, Subscription Manager, faculty free to teach from any number of books (perlego.com/institutions).)
2. IF in_tam AND no Tier-1 competitor_platform AND reading_list_gap = high_cost_required_texts (syllabus or bookstore required list still selling individual textbooks) -> Tier 2 - Instructor Pilot Brief (The department still sends students to buy individual titles. Maps to the Instructors Pilot Program (perlego.com/instructors).)
3. IF in_tam AND no Tier-1 competitor_platform AND no Tier-2 reading_list_gap AND affordability_page = textbook_affordability_or_wp_or_edi_page_with_no_unlimited_library_named -> Tier 3 - Access & Equity Brief (The institution publicly cares about access and still has no unlimited digital library named. Homepage meta: Save up to 90% on textbooks.)
4. IF in_tam AND none of the above -> Catch-all - Institutional Library Playbook (Still in TAM (universities, colleges, academic departments). Softest CTA. Mandatory 100% coverage.)

Tiers

Each course-materials state gets a different first offer

Competitor inclusive-access stacks get a Campus Switch Brief. High-cost reading lists get an Instructor Pilot Brief. Affordability/WP pages with no unlimited library get an Access & Equity Brief. The rest get the Institutional Library Playbook.
