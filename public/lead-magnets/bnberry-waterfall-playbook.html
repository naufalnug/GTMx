Signal

The signal is how the hotel already shows up on lodging marketplaces

Booking.com/Expedia presence, Airbnb/Vrbo listing presence, and a named channel manager on BuiltWith already tell you whether the new-channel offer, the full-service switch, or guest messaging should lead. That stack-state is stronger than a title guess, and it is cheap to collect.

Waterfall

One cascade routes every in-TAM hotel and resort

First match wins. On Booking/Expedia but missing Airbnb/Vrbo goes first, then Airbnb/Vrbo listings sitting on a competitor CM, then live marketplace listings with weak guest-reply / review response, then every remaining independent hotel or small resort group.

first match wins 
1. IF in_tam AND ota_surface = on_booking_or_expedia AND airbnb_vrbo_state = not_listed -> Tier 1 - New Channel Brief (They already sell rooms on traditional OTAs but are missing the Airbnb/Vrbo traveler. Strongest intent for BnBerry's core offer: Connect your Hotel to Airbnb and Vrbo and see immediate ROI; Forest Suites quote on homepage about Airbnb as a growing segment as STR legislation cuts inventory.)
2. IF in_tam AND airbnb_vrbo_state = listed AND competitor_cm detected (SiteMinder, Cloudbeds, Guesty, Hostaway, or another named CM / Airbnb connector) -> Tier 2 - Full-Service Switch Brief (Inventory is already on Airbnb/Vrbo through a generic CM or DIY connector. Pain is hotel-native listing optimization + 24/7 marketplace guest support (connectivity page: Airbnb/Vrbo traffic is 10x other OTAs). BnBerry's full-service / DIY portal is the upgrade, not a lecture about what Airbnb is.)
3. IF in_tam AND airbnb_vrbo_state = listed AND no Tier-2 competitor_cm AND review_gap = weak_or_slow_reply (slow Airbnb/Vrbo guest reply or weak review response visible on the listing) -> Tier 3 - Guest Messaging Brief (The listing is live but guest-comms are the leak. Homepage Guest Messaging pitch: stay connected, generate 5-star reviews, grow bookings without hiring; resolve issues by text. Maps to the SMS / review offer, not a new-channel sell.)
4. IF in_tam AND none of the above -> Catch-all - 3D Tour / Extra Distribution Playbook (Still in TAM (independent hotels and small hotel/resort groups). Softest CTA around 3D/360 tours and broader marketplace distribution. Mandatory 100% coverage.)

Missing Airbnb/Vrbo gets a New Channel Brief. Competitor-CM Airbnb/Vrbo listings get a Full-Service Switch Brief. Weak guest-reply listings get a Guest Messaging Brief. The rest get the 3D Tour / Extra Distribution Playbook.
