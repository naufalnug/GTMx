Prepared for Baicells Technologies

Your Baicells waterfall journey.

One routed cascade that finds operators and service providers with a live reason to build or upgrade RAN, and hands each one the Baicells proof that fits.

Core insight: Every RAN vendor pitches coverage and price. The operators who will actually buy are the ones with a public reason to spend on radio now: a fresh spectrum award or rollout obligation, a network still stuck on 2G/3G/4G, or a fixed-wireless business that lives or dies on cost per subscriber. Route on that reason, lead with 'Connect More with Less', and let the catch-all carry private-network and vertical buyers.

Signal

The signal is a public reason to spend on radio access now

Spectrum awards, rollout obligations, legacy-only networks and fixed-wireless offers are all visible in regulator filings, operator websites and coverage data. No paid intent data needed.

strongest signal
01

Spectrum awards, licence renewals and rollout obligations are published by national regulators, so a time-bound reason to buy radio is public and dated.

02

An operator's own site, press and coverage maps (Ookla, nPerf, GSMA coverage) show whether it has commercial 5G yet, which tells you if a 4G-to-5G upgrade story lands.

03

Fixed-wireless and WISP operators advertise FWA plans and coverage areas on their homepages, so the cost-per-subscriber pain is visible from a simple scrape.

04

All signals come with the TAM pull (regulator licence registries, operator directories, homepage scrape), so routing does not need expensive enrichment.

Waterfall

First match wins, top to bottom, catch-all underneath

Exclude existing Baicells customers and channel partners, then route: fresh spectrum award or rollout obligation, then no commercial 5G yet, then fixed-wireless / WISP operators, then every other operator, ISP and private-network buyer. Each account lands in exactly one campaign.

first match wins 
1. IF baicells_customer = true OR channel_partner = true -> Exclude (Already buying from or reselling Baicells. Route to account management / channel, not cold outbound.)
2. IF spectrum_award_12m = true OR rollout_obligation = true -> Tier 1: Spectrum award / rollout deadline (A dated, public obligation to build coverage. Strongest, most time-bound intent.)
3. IF ELSE IF operator_type in {MNO, ISP} AND commercial_5g_live = false -> Tier 2: 5G upgrade path (Network still on 2G/3G/4G. Baicells radios such as Nova452 upgrade from 4G to 5G by software, and Fusion RAN runs 2G/4G/5G from one platform.)
4. IF ELSE IF fwa_plans_advertised = true OR operator_type = WISP -> Tier 3: Fixed wireless economics (FWA business depends on cost per base station and per CPE. Baicells sells both base stations and 4G/5G CPEs with low cost of entry.)
5. IF ELSE (every remaining operator, ISP and enterprise / vertical account in the TAM) -> Tier 4: Catch-all, private LTE/5G and 'Connect More with Less' (Covers 100% of the TAM. Broad private-network and price-performance angle, softest ask.)
