The signal

The address a practitioner publishes for themselves

It is a complete diagnostic of their relationship with physical space - and joined to NYC land-use records, it becomes a field no competitor can buy.

strongest signal
01

It cannot be bought. It is a join between federal provider records and municipal land-use records - no vendor sells it, and no other Brooklyn operator has built it.

02

It costs nothing. Forty-six thousand accounts can be scored without spending a single enrichment credit, so the unit economics hold at any scale.

03

It is published under legal mandate, and it updates continuously. CMS republishes the national provider file weekly; New York issues new licenses every month.

04

The market refills itself. Every newly licensed practitioner in New York lands in Tier 4 automatically. Most outbound lists decay - this one has an intake pipe.
