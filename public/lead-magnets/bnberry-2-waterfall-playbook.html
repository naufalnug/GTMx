Your rebuilt BnBerry waterfall journey.

A scrollable GTMx strategy artifact rebuilt from the discovery call: rating-qualified boutique hotels that are not on Airbnb or Vrbo yet, split by single property vs management company, two-step sequences, 2,000-3,000 sends a day. Prepared for Fiona Taylor, Global Director.

Core insight: Fiona's own read is the whole strategy: the top 30% of BnBerry's hotels drive effectively all revenue and the bottom 70% produce nothing, and with no historical CRM data there was never a filter to tell the two apart before signing. So the list is not 'hotels' - it is producer probability, assembled from four public fields before a single email sends: no Airbnb/Vrbo listing yet, a Booking.com rating of 7.8 or better, kitchenette / villa / suite-style inventory, and a location on Airbnb's top-300 priority list. Portfolio size is the only second cut, because a single property is a channel launch and a management company is a rollout - genuinely different offers. Everything else (title, brand, key count) is a merge tag, not a tier. This is also what pays for the pricing change: 2% plus $200/month only works if the accounts you sign actually produce, which means the qualification bar has to move upstream into list building rather than sit with the AE.

Waterfall

One cascade routes every in-TAM property, independent or managed

First match wins. Priority-market villa stock goes first, then rating-qualified independents, then management companies as a rollout, then properties already live on Airbnb, then everything else still in TAM.

This is the profile behind the top 30% of revenue.)
2. IF IF in_tam AND chain_flag = false AND portfolio_size = single_property AND airbnb_vrbo_state = not_listed AND booking_rating >= 7.8 AND no Tier-1 priority_market match -> Tier 2 - Rating-Qualified New Channel Brief (Same core ICP and the same offer, one market rung down. This is the volume core of the programme: rated, unlisted, single-property boutique hotels outside the top-300 markets.)
3. IF IF in_tam AND chain_flag = false AND portfolio_size = management_company_or_group AND at least one property has airbnb_vrbo_state = not_listed -> Tier 3 - Portfolio Rollout Brief (Separate campaign, separate offer. One conversation opens several properties, so the ask is a single pilot property with a rollout behind it - not a one-building channel launch. Small management companies only; brand HQs stay excluded.)
4. IF IF in_tam AND chain_flag = false AND airbnb_vrbo_state = listed -> Tier 4 - Full-Service Switch / Guest Messaging Brief (Outside the stated ICP but not worthless. Inventory is live through a connector or a DIY setup, and the property still owns the guest-comms load Airbnb and Vrbo generate at roughly 10x other OTAs, plus listing optimization a thin path skips.)
5. IF IF in_tam AND none of the above (including booking_rating below 7.8) -> Catch-all - 3D Tour / Extra Distribution Playbook (Still a lodging property with a reachable decision maker, but below the producer bar. Softest CTA, lowest priority, mandatory for 100% coverage - and a cheap way to keep testing whether the 7.8 floor is set correctly.)
