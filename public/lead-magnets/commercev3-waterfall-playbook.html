01

The signal nobody can buy

AI-visibility state is measurable on every account for free, it maps one-to-one onto the CommerceV3 service ladder, and no competitor can purchase the list underneath it.

strongest signal
01

It is unbuyable. No provider sells the field "is this brand cited in ChatGPT for its category's buying questions". Apollo cannot, Clay cannot, ZoomInfo cannot. The list is structurally unavailable to every competitor.

02

It is free. Query the answer engines, parse the cited domains, read the prospect's own robots.txt and page source. No per-record cost, so the economics hold at 40,000 accounts exactly as well as at 400.

03

The signal is the lead magnet. The same sweep that assigns a tier also produces the personalised artifact offered in the email. One pipeline, two jobs, marginal cost of a bespoke magnet near zero.

04

It is undeniable. "You appear in zero of the ten buying questions in your category, your competitor appears in six." The prospect can verify it in thirty seconds by opening ChatGPT, which is exactly the behaviour we want.

02

How the market gets routed

Every account is tested top to bottom and lands in the first tier it matches. No account occupies two tiers, no account occupies zero, and the catch-all guarantees complete coverage.

first match wins 
1. IF robots.txt blocks GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot or Google-Extended AND the brand is cited in zero of the ten category questions -> Tier 1 - Locked Out (They are excluded from the fastest-growing discovery surface in commerce by a configuration file nobody has opened since the theme was installed. Mechanically true, instantly fixable, and almost always news to them.)
2. IF Not Tier 1, AND zero citations, AND two or more named direct competitors are cited across the same ten questions -> Tier 2 - Beaten to the Answer (This is not a traffic problem, it is a recommendation problem, and it has a name attached to it. The strongest emotional trigger in the cascade.)
3. IF Not Tier 1 or 2, AND at least one citation, AND the citation is sourced from third parties OR there is no Product schema OR there is no llms.txt -> Tier 3 - Cited but Uncontrolled (The engines describe the brand using somebody else's words. The presence is accidental, unowned, and one competitor push away from vanishing.)
4. IF Not Tier 1 to 3, AND ten or more active Meta creatives, AND either no testing tool detected OR fewer than three indexable pages per SKU -> Tier 4 - Buying Demand You Cannot Convert (Auction-priced traffic landing on a page nobody has tested and a catalog with no long-tail surface. The leak is downstream of the spend, which is why the spend keeps looking like the problem.)
5. IF Everything else in the qualified TAM -> Tier 5 - Stack Tax (catch-all) (A competent operation spread across five to eight vendors, each with an invoice, a reporting cadence and a share of the blame. The coordination tax stays invisible until somebody totals it.)

The campaign set

Five core campaigns inside the waterfall plus four opt-in side campaigns triggered by orthogonal events. The side campaigns run only once the cascade is stable.

volume + side quests
Core Niche

Crawler Lockout

The most urgent and smallest tier. The pitch writes itself because the problem is mechanical rather than debatable, and the fix takes fifteen minutes while the citations take ninety days. Burn this list early, because brands do eventually fix robots.txt without us.

Filter
robots.txt disallows at least one major AI crawler AND zero citations across the ten category questions.
AI strategy
Fetch robots.txt on every domain in the TAM and diff it against the current list of AI user agents. Pair the blocked-bot list with that account's citation sweep so the email can state both the cause and the effect in two sentences.
Value
Fix the cause
Core Focused

Category Answer Map

The flagship. This tier converts on a specific, verifiable, slightly humiliating fact that the reader can confirm in their own ChatGPT window in thirty seconds. Launch first, capped at 1,500 contacts while the named-proof gap is being closed.

Filter
Zero citations while two or more named direct competitors appear across the same ten buying questions.
AI strategy
Run the ten standard buying questions for the account's vertical against four answer engines, record every cited domain, and rank competitors by citation share. The email names the two strongest rivals and the count of questions each of them won.
Value
Make money
Core Focused

Entity Control

Sells defensibility rather than discovery. A citation you do not own is a citation you can lose, and you will not get an alert when a competitor publishes properly structured content against the same question. Needs the sharpest artifact in the set, so it launches after the report pipeline has matured.

Filter
Cited at least once, but from third-party sources, or missing Product schema, or missing llms.txt.
AI strategy
Capture the actual sentence the engines use to describe the brand and the URL they cite it from, then diff the site's JSON-LD against the schema types the category needs. The email quotes the description back to them.
Value
Protect the position
Core Focused

Ad-to-Answer Leak

Ties spend the prospect has already approved to a leak they have not quantified. Every point of conversion rate applies to 100% of the traffic they are already paying for, which is the only lever that improves paid, organic and AI-referred revenue simultaneously.

Filter
Ten or more active Meta creatives, and either no testing tool detected or fewer than three indexable pages per SKU.
AI strategy
Pull the live creative count from the Meta Ad Library, check the landing paths for any testing-tool script, and compute the sitemap-to-SKU ratio. Two independently verified halves: spend is real, and the conversion layer underneath it is not there.
Value
Save money

Launch sequence

Tier 2 opens at capped volume to prove the flagship signal, Tier 1 follows while it is still fresh, and the catch-all becomes the permanent volume engine from week ten.

launch first
01

Week 0 - Run the citation sweep clean across a 200-account sample and confirm artifact generation end to end in under four working hours. If the sweep is not reliable, nothing downstream is.

02

Week 0 - Stand up the reply-handling procedure: who sends the artifact, in what format, and what the follow-up is once it has been opened. The artifact goes out as a link, not an attachment, with no call request attached.

03

Week 0 - Clear at least one named-brand proof point for use in cold email, or make an explicit decision to launch without one. CommerceV3 currently publishes strong aggregate numbers and zero named customers.

04

Week 0 - Warm sending infrastructure to 2,000 contacts a week. Open tracking off, reply and link tracking on.

05

Weeks 1 to 2 - Launch Tier 2, Beaten to the Answer, capped at 1,500 contacts. Strongest artifact and clearest pain, deliberately capped while the named-proof gap closes.

06

Weeks 3 to 4 - Launch Tier 1, Locked Out, at the full 4,800 contacts. Smallest and most urgent tier, worth burning while it is fresh because brands do fix robots.txt.

07

Weeks 5 to 6 - Launch Tier 4, Buying Demand, at 2,000 a week. Different buyer motivation, which diversifies the reply mix away from AI visibility alone.

08

Weeks 7 to 9 - Launch Tier 3, Cited but Uncontrolled, at 2,000 a week, once the report pipeline has matured enough to produce the sharpest artifact in the set.

09

Week 10 onward - Open Tier 5, Stack Tax, on remaining capacity as the permanent volume engine running underneath everything else.

10

Every 90 days - Re-run the citation sweep across the whole TAM. Accounts move up as they fix things and down as competitors overtake them, regenerating fresh personalised outreach without buying a single new lead.

Scale / cut rules

1 positive reply per 150 contacts Perfect. Scale the tier hard and pull volume forward from later phases.
1 per 300 Good. Let it run untouched and do not fiddle with the copy.
1 per 450 to 500 Borderline. Test one copy variant only, varying either the lead magnet or the notice line, never both at once.
Worse than 1 per 500, or reply rate under 1% after 1,500 sends Dead. Cut the tier and rework the trigger rather than rewriting the email.
Two copy variants per tier, maximum A fresh tier starts with a chaos test of two genuinely different emails. Once a winner emerges, switch to scientific tests holding everything constant but one variable.
